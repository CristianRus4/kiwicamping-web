# KiwiCamping website brief

## Purpose

KiwiCamping helps travellers explore New Zealand places, save the ones that matter and turn them into complete road trips. The website presents the current iPhone app and publishes genuinely useful New Zealand travel information.

## Verified product facts

- More than 4,500 bundled place records.
- More than 2,000 places to stay across campsites, huts, holiday parks and other bookable accommodation.
- Core place details remain available offline. Live maps, weather, routing and external pages can require reception.
- Map, list and satellite exploration with detailed place and facility filters.
- Liked, Starred, Visited, Want to Visit and custom saved collections.
- Trips with ordered stops, dates, notes, total route distance, visited progress and to-dos.
- Place weather, selected-currency costs and Apple Look Around where available.
- The five website testimonials remain identical to the current app paywall.

Do not invent App Store ratings, prices, booking availability, review counts or complete offline map coverage.

## Brand and interface

- Product name: `KiwiCamping`.
- Paywall green runs from `#507A49` to `#1F4728`; the website uses the darker end as its core green.
- Use DM Sans throughout, Lucide and React Icons for interface symbols, square geometry, real photography and real app screenshots.
- Never generate illustrations, self-drawn SVG icons or article imagery.
- Support light and dark colour schemes with the same background on the fixed navigation bar and body.

## Editorial rules

Write in direct, practical New Zealand English. Name real places, road decisions, rule owners and tradeoffs. Keep every article's language distinct. Each article must contain at least ten substantive prose paragraphs before a short numbered summary, a pull quote, its own photograph, related reading and a natural app call to action.

Never explain the website's content model to the visitor. Avoid generic SEO filler, developer notes, launch caveats, em dashes and unsupported absolutes. Current signs, official alerts and the responsible authority always outrank an app listing or old article.

## Site structure

- Homepage with product workflow, current app screenshots, features, routes, paywall reviews, FAQ and App Store QR.
- 74 guides across road trips, camping, rules and safety, planning, app tutorials, and costs and budgets.
- Currency converter and fuel-cost calculator.
- Support, privacy and terms.
- Complete German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese editions.
- Sitemap, robots and `llms.txt` crawler surfaces.

## Publishing

The rebuild remains local on branch `neue`. Do not push or deploy until the app update containing the advertised features is public and the owner explicitly approves release.
