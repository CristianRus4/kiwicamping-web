Optional, not errors:
- Section 1 para 1: "for specified periods" is narrower than the Act, which says "either generally or for one or more specified periods". Suggested: "at all times or for specified periods".
- Section 1 para 1: s 170 requires the constable, before searching, to tell the person they can remove the container or vehicle from the area. Optional add: "after first giving you the chance to take it out of the area".
- Mauao section: tone is respectful and nothing is overstated about the disaster. Optionally acknowledge it plainly: "the Mauao landslide of 22 January 2026, in which six people died".
- Tauranga row: Papamoa's permanent area runs 9pm to 7am daily, not 24/7. The row does not say otherwise, but "Permanent bans include ... Papamoa" readers may assume 24/7. Optional: "Papamoa's seaward beaches and reserves are alcohol-free 9pm to 7am daily".

## 1. Liquor ban table, row by row

### Thames-Coromandel
- 4pm 23 December to 4pm 6 January, mapped areas: CONFIRMED. https://www.tcdc.govt.nz/alcoholcontrol (WebFetch; curl gets 403). Schedule One Part B: "From 4pm on 23 December to 4pm on 6 January each year"; extension to 4am Monday only if 6 January is a Friday to Sunday. 6 January 2027 is a Wednesday, so no extension.
- Permanent bans in mapped areas at all times: CONFIRMED (TCDC alcohol bans page: "24 hours a day, seven days a week, 365 days a year").
- Self-contained exemption (clause 7.3): CONFIRMED. Conditions: consumed inside the vehicle; used for freedom camping in a designated freedom camping area; occupants do not exceed the certificate's maximum.

## 2. Local Government Act 2002 and drink-driving

- s 147 (DLM172980): CONFIRMED, with optional wording tweak ("either generally or for one or more specified periods"; consumption, bringing in, possession; s 147(3) covers vehicles).
- s 169 (DLM173433): CONFIRMED. A constable may without warrant search a container in the possession of a person in or entering a restricted place, or a vehicle in or entering it; seize alcohol; arrest a person committing an offence. s 170 adds a pre-search condition (optional mention).
- Police limits: CONFIRMED. https://www.police.govt.nz/advice-services/driving-and-road-safety/being-safe-road-rules-and-reasons : "250 micrograms of alcohol per litre of breath", "50 milligrams of alcohol per 100 millilitres of blood", under 20 "The limit is zero."
