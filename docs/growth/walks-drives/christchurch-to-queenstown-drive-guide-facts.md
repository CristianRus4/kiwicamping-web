## Distances and times
- Visit Mackenzie, Getting here and around. URL https://www.mackenzienz.com/getting-here-and-around/ . "just 2.5 hours drive south of Christchurch and approximately 2.5 hours drive north of Queenstown" (the Mackenzie region). "Traveling south by car from Christchurch takes approximately three hours to Lake Tekapo, and Aoraki/Mount Cook is one hour further from here. Allow more time if you plan to stop at the rural towns Fairlie and Geraldine". Times: Fairlie to Lake Tekapo 30 minutes; Lake Tekapo to Twizel 45 minutes; Lake Tekapo to Aoraki/Mount Cook Village 1 hour 15 minutes; Aoraki/Mount Cook Village to Twizel 45 minutes. Petrol: "BP service station in Fairlie located on Main Street, heading towards Lake Tekapo"; "Challenge service station at the north end of town" (Tekapo); "Mobil station in Twizel on Mackenzie Drive in the centre of town"; "self-service petrol pump at Aoraki/Mount Cook (credit card only)". "In winter, snow and ice can cover roads, and the high country location means a few up and down hill slopes and winding sections."
- Tourism Waitaki, Getting here. URL https://www.waitakinz.com/about/getting-here . Christchurch to Oamaru 3 hours 30 min (248 km), SH1; Christchurch to Omarama 4-hour drive (345 km), "through the Mackenzie Basin via State Highway 8"; Omarama to Oamaru 1 hour 30 min (118 km) via SH83 Waitaki Valley. "Queenstown Airport: Drive 3.5 hours North via SH8 then SH83 at Omarama to Oamaru." Omarama page https://www.waitakinz.com/destinations/omarama : "a crossroads between Queenstown, Twizel, and Waitaki"; Paritea Clay Cliffs "From SH8 make your way along to Henburn Road. Once parked, there is a 15-minute walk".
- Lake Wanaka Tourism, Driving to Wanaka. URL https://www.wanaka.co.nz/plan-your-trip/getting-to-wanaka/driving-to-wanaka/ . Queenstown to Wanaka via Crown Range Road 1 hour ("Chains may be required in winter"); via Cromwell (State Highway 6) 1 hour 15 minutes ("slightly longer but more gradual route, recommended during poor weather or for those towing trailers or campervans"); Christchurch to Wanaka 5.5 hours. FAQ: "In winter (May-October), snow chains must be carried when travelling the Crown Range Road. QLDC recommends carrying chains all winter..."; "69km drive"; "Large campervans and motorhomes can use the Crown Range Road, but it requires confident mountain driving skills ... not advised for towing vehicles or vehicles longer than about 12 metres". The FAQ cites QLDC and Wanaka App; the page is a tourism-body page, not QLDC.
- DOC Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park, Getting there. URL https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/canterbury/places/aoraki-mount-cook-national-park/getting-there/ . "accessed off State Highway 8 at the end of State Highway 80. It is a 3.5 hour drive from Queenstown and a 4.5 hour drive from Christchurch."
- Destination Queenstown, Getting here. URL https://www.queenstownnz.co.nz/plan/getting-here-and-getting-around/ . "We drive on the left-hand side of the road"; "New Zealand roads can be narrow and winding with varying conditions. It pays to take lots of breaks and leave plenty of time to reach your destination."; "ice or snow on the roads in winter".

## Page arithmetic (labelled on the page)
- Omarama to Queenstown about 2 hours: Tourism Waitaki Queenstown Airport to Oamaru 3.5 h minus Omarama to Oamaru 1.5 h. Christchurch to Omarama 4 h + 2 h = about 6 h.
- Via Wanaka: 5.5 h + 1 h (Crown Range) = 6.5 h.
- Via Oamaru to Omarama: 248 + 118 = 366 km, 3.5 + 1.5 = 5 h, against 345 km and 4 h direct.
