# Paparoa Track 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21, lane max-k-walks)

## DOC Paparoa Track page (curl + text extraction)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/west-coast/places/paparoa-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/paparoa-track/ (short URL doc.govt.nz/paparoatrack serves the same page)
- "55 km one way", "3 days one way" (walking, Intermediate), "2 days one way, Grade 4" (biking). "Time: 3 days walking, 2 days mountain biking (no e-bikes). Distance: 55 km walking, 56. 2 km mountain biking". "No dogs." "Bookings are open for the 2026/27 season."
- Huts: "There are three Great Walk huts on the Paparoa Track: Ces Clark Hut, Moonlight Tops Hut and Pororari Hut. Huts have bunks, mattresses, heating, gas cooktops, toilets, and a water supply. A DOC ranger may be present. The huts do not provide food, cooking utensils or showers." "There are no campsites on the track." "You'll need accommodation the night before your walk - camping at Smoke-ho car park is not permitted." Bunks: Ces Clark 16, Moonlight Tops 20, Pororari 20 (cooking, heating, mattresses, toilets non-flush, water from tap not treated, boil before use; Moonlight and Pororari also lighting). Gear list: "hut bunkrooms don't have heating and can be cold" (contradicts the facilities line; the guide reports both). "You cannot buy food on the track."
- Backcountry Hut Passes: usable at Ces Clark (book online, then email paparoavc@doc.govt.nz with booking reference and scanned pass for a refund); not at Moonlight Tops or Pororari.
- Options: 1 or 2 nights to Pororari Hut see Pororari Hut Track; 2 nights Moonlight Tops + Pororari; "It's not feasible to stay at Ces Clark Hut and Pororari Hut - it's 28.8 km and a 10 hr walk"; 3 nights all three. Pike29 Memorial Track can be used alongside.
- Walking sections: Smoke-ho car park to Ces Clark 4-5 hr 10.3 km ("much rougher ... historic nature"); Ces Clark to Moonlight Tops 3 hr 9.7 km; Moonlight Tops to Pororari 5-7 hr 19.1 km; Pororari Hut to Pororari River car park 16 km or Waikori Road car park 17.1 km, 4-5 hr. Walkers exit to Pororari River car park; "Mountain bikers must exit out the Punakaiki River Valley to Waikori Road car park." Croesus Top Hut: "you cannot stay at Croesus Top Hut ... in an emergency, you can use it for shelter."
- Biking: Smoke-ho to Ces Clark 2-3 hr; Ces Clark to Moonlight 1 hr 30-2 hr 30; Moonlight to Pororari 4-5 hr; Pororari to Waikori Road 2 hr 30-3 hr 30; Smoke-ho to Pike29 car park 37 km, 6 hr 30-9 hr 30. "Advanced: Grade 4". "Plan to spend one night on the track." Rules: "E-bikes are not allowed ... prohibited under the Paparoa National Park Management Plan." "Riding at night is not allowed, to protect nocturnal species." Shared use.
- Fees (page tables): Ces Clark Oct-Apr NZ $50 / INT $76, child $25 / $38; 1 May-30 Sep $50 / $50, $25 / $25. Moonlight Tops Oct-Apr $78 / $117, child $39 / $59; 1 May-30 Jun $61 / $61, child $30; 1 Jul-31 Aug $50 / $50, $25; Sep $61 / $61, $30. Pororari Oct-Apr $61 / $92, child $31 / $46; 1 May-30 Sep $50 / $50, $25. Infant NZ 0-4 free. "Proof of eligibility is required for the New Zealand rate." "There are no fees to complete a day walk/ride on the track or for entry into Paparoa National Park." The pricing page spells "Ces Clarke" and agrees. Three-night sum (Oct-Apr adult): NZ 50+78+61 = $189, INT 76+117+92 = $285 (computed).
- Booking: "All three huts must be booked in advance. If you don't have a valid booking ... penalty, or turned away". Nearest towns Blackball, Greymouth, Punakaiki. Access: Smoke-ho car park at end of Blackball Road, 8 km north of Blackball; Pororari River Track carpark SH6 1.2 km north of Pancake Rocks; Waikori Road carpark SH6 2.4 km south of Pancake Rocks. Transport: "Several operators provide shuttle services"; link to West Coast Tourism. "We recommend leaving your car at your accommodation - Smoke-ho car park is small and for day use only." Car break-ins reported at Smoko, Waikori Road and Pororari River car parks.
- Weather: "annual rainfall averaging around 6,000 mm"; heavy rain, snow, ice, fog, strong winds any time; Pororari Valley sections may be impassable in very heavy rain; landslides and rock falls; "no cellphone coverage on most of the Paparoa Track"; Ongaonga. Kaitiaki: Ngāti Waewae hapū of Ngāi Tahu. History: Croesus Track 1881-1899; Pike River explosion 19 November 2010, 29 men; track a memorial created with families, DOC and Ngāti Waewae.
- Season dates: none stated on the page.

## DOC Paparoa Track guide (PDF, pdftotext)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/parks-and-recreation/tracks-and-walks/west-coast/paparoa-track-guide.pdf
- "55.1 km hiking (one way), 56.2 km mountain biking". "It is best to hike or mountain bike the Paparoa Track from south to north, starting at Smoke-ho car park near Blackball and finishing at Punakaiki." (a recommendation; no rule found on the track page). "Bikers must give way to walkers." Mountain biking "year-round during daylight hours only (from half an hour before sunrise to half an hour after sunset)". Rules: no drones, "No fires, except in fireplaces inside huts", no dogs, "No camping within 500 m of the track", no e-bikes, no hunting within 500 m.

## DOC Pike29 Memorial Track page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/west-coast/places/paparoa-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/pike29-memorial-track/
- 11.6 km one way; walking 5-6 hr uphill, 3 hr 30-4 hr 30 downhill; biking 3 hr 30-4 hr uphill, 1-2 hr downhill; Grade 4; climbs about 800 m. Road: from Greymouth Taylorville Road, Taylorville-Blackball Road (becomes Atarau Road), Logburn Road 15 km north-east of Blackball (Pike Valley Road); narrow, windy gravel; "buses and long campervans are not recommended".
