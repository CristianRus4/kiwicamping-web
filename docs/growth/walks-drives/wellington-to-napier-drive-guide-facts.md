## Camping
- DOC Holdsworth Campsite https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/wellington-kapiti/places/tararua-forest-park/things-to-do/campsites/holdsworth-campsite/ : Standard; bookings required; 150 non-powered/tent sites; fires in designated fire places only if no fire ban; dogs on a leash only; adult $10, child $5, infant free; maximum seven days per stay; "2 km south of Masterton, turn off SH2 at signpost to Mt Holdsworth, Norfolk Rd. ... The campsite is 15 km from SH turnoff. Sealed road access, gravel carpark." "Note the road into Holdsworth Campsite has been damaged by weather events, so take care."
- DOC Kiriwhakapapa Campsite https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/wellington-kapiti/places/tararua-forest-park/things-to-do/campsites/kiriwhakapapa-campsite/ : Standard; bookings required; 15 non-powered/tent sites; fires not allowed at any time; dogs on a leash only; adult $10, child $5, infant free; "Turn off SH2, 15 km north of Masterton onto Kiriwhakapapa Rd, and follow 7 km to road end. 5 km of gravel road."
- Napier City Council freedom camping page https://www.napier.govt.nz/napier/freedom-camping/information/ : "Our bylaw allows certified self-contained vehicles only to park anywhere in the Napier district that is not prohibited, for one night only." Sites first come first served, self-contained only: Pump Track (Ellison Street) carpark, three nights max; Te Karaka (Perfume Point) carpark, four vehicles in designated spaces, two nights; Marine Parade carpark (northern end), four vehicles, two nights; National Aquarium of New Zealand carpark, four vehicles between 5.30pm and 7.30am only, two nights. "All drivers of non-self-contained vehicles must find alternative accommodation." Holiday parks listed: Kennedy Park Resort, Westshore Holiday Park, Bay View Hotel and Holiday Park, Napier Beach Top 10 Holiday Park, NZMCA park Meeanee (members only). "Self-contained vehicles must clearly display a green sticker to indicate compliance with the NZ Standard NZS5465:2001". "No person may freedom camp for more than 10 nights in any rolling 30 day period on any land controlled or managed by Napier City Council." Fines under Freedom Camping Act 2011. "Napier has three public dump stations".
- Masterton District Council https://www.mstn.govt.nz/recreation/camping/freedom-camping : "If you want to access freedom camping areas you need a fully self-contained vehicle that includes a toilet, shower and wastewater facilities." Overnight parking for self-contained vehicles only at Henley Lake off Colombo Road, maximum stay two nights. One dump station in the district, at Mawley Holiday Park, free in office hours. The page's DOC fee lines ($8 adult) disagree with DOC's current $10, so the page is treated as possibly dated: only the rules above are used and the page says to check.
- KiwiCamping counts (lib/data/hubs.json, data 2026-08-11): Wairarapa camping hub 6 DOC campsites, campervan access listed for 6, largest Holdsworth (150); Hawke's Bay camping hub 7 DOC campsites, campervan access 2, 6 free, largest Kuripapango (Ox Bow) (50); Wellington/Kapiti hub 3.
