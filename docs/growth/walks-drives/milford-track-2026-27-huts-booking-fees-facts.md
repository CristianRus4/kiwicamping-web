## DOC Milford Track page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/milford-track/ (fetched with curl, HTTP 200)
- "53.5 km one way", "4 days". "The Milford Track can only be walked in one direction over 3 nights and 4 days."
- Season: "1 November 2026 - 30 April 2027 last departure 28 April 2027". "Bookings are required for huts and transport."
- "There are three huts on the Milford Track, which must be booked well in advance during the Great Walks season. Camping is not permitted on the Milford Track."
- "Huts have bunks, mattresses, heating, toilets, basic cooking facilities, solar-powered lighting and cold running water. A DOC ranger is in residence. The huts do not provide cooking utensils or showers."
- Clinton, Mintaro, Dumpling: each "40 bunk beds, cooking, heating, lighting, mattresses, toilets - flush, water from tap - not treated, boil before use".
- Day 1 Glade Wharf to Clinton Hut 1 to 1 hr 30 min, 5 km, after "boat cruise from Te Anau Downs (1 hour 15 minutes)". Day 2 Clinton to Mintaro 6 hr, 17.5 km. Day 3 Mintaro to Dumpling 6 to 7 hr, 13 km; Omanui/McKinnon Pass Shelter (1154 m); "climb over 500 m ... descend 1000 m". Day 4 Dumpling to Sandfly Point 5 hr 30 min to 6 hr, 18 km, "in time to meet the early afternoon boat".
- Side trips: Wetland Walkway 15 min return; Sutherland Falls 1 hr 30 min return, "drop 580 m in 3 leaps".
- Fees (adult NZ / INT / child NZ 5 to 17 / child INT 0 to 17 / infant NZ 0 to 4): 1 Nov to 30 Apr $106 / $152 / $53 / $76 / free; 1 May to 30 Jun $30 / $30 / $15 / $15; 1 Jul to 31 Oct $30 / $30 / $15 / $15. "Proof of eligibility is required for the New Zealand rate."
- Bookings: "Bookings are open for the 2026/27 season." In season "Book Milford Track huts online. If you don't have a valid booking for your stay, you may be charged a penalty, or turned away if the hut is full." Outside season (1 May to 31 Oct 2026) "Bookings are not required. Bunks are first come, first served. Payment is with Backcountry Hut Tickets or a Backcountry Hut Pass".
- Transport: "From Te Anau, take a bus or private transport to Te Anau Downs (30 minutes), then a boat trip to Glade Wharf ... (1 hour 15 minutes)." "The track finishes at Sandfly Point on Milford Sound. Take a boat trip (20 minutes) to Milford Sound/Piopiotahi, where there are bus services back to Te Anau and on to Queenstown." "Transport must be booked in advance. There is no vehicle or private access to the start/end of the track." "During the Great Walks season, full and partial transport packages are available from one of these independent operators." Link names: Cheeky Kiwi, Easyhike, Fiordland Outdoors Company, Tracknet (listed again in the winter section). "During winter, transport operators require minimum numbers and do not always operate a daily service."
- Guided: only "Consider guided walks on the Milford Track (Visit Fiordland website)" with a link to https://www.fiordland.org.nz/things-to-do/walking-and-hiking/guided-walks/. DOC says nothing else about guided versus independent walkers. The Visit Fiordland page renders client-side; nothing Milford-specific could be read, so no guided facts are on the page.
- Avalanche: "The risk of avalanche can extend into December. During the Great Walks season DOC manages this risk to a low level and walkers may be flown (at their cost) over dangerous sections."
- "very high rainfall (up to 9000 mm per year)"; "There is no cellphone coverage."; "not recommended for children under the age of 10"; didymo in Lake Te Anau and Milford Sound, "currently not in the pristine Clinton and Arthur rivers"; "You cannot buy food on the track."; "We do not provide emergency food in huts."
- Outside season: "Advanced and expert track categories"; "DOC does not manage hazards such as flooding and avalanches"; "Many of the bridges across side streams are removed over winter".
