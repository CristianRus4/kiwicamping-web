# Hump Ridge Track 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21, lane max-k-walks)

Operator: the Trust's own site humpridgetrack.co.nz was NOT blocked (HTTP 200 to curl). The Trust's booking cart pages were not used. The Trust's brand is "Tuatapere Humpridge Track"; DOC and the Trust's pages call the owner the Tuatapere Hump Track Charitable Trust ("Hump Ridge Trust" on DOC); the operating company is "Tuatapere Hump Track Ltd" / "Tuatapere Hump Track Limited".

## DOC Hump Ridge Track page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/hump-ridge-track/
- "61.5 km loop", "3 days/2 nights", "Intermediate tramping track", "No dogs." "opened as our 11th Great Walk on 25 October 2024." "This Great Walk is a partnership between the Tuatapere Hump Track Charitable Trust and DOC. The track and lodges are maintained and operated by the Trust." "The lodges are operated by the Trust and offer a higher standard of accommodation than other Great Walk huts."
- Fitness: "moderate to high fitness ... best suited to people who have experience doing overnight hikes"; "walk for 7-9 hours in a day". "Unless you arrange helicopter pack transport, you need to carry all your food and gear. Meals can also be purchased in the lodges."
- Day 1 Rarakau car park to Ōkaka Lodge 20.6 km (7-9 hr); Day 2 Ōkaka to Port Craig Lodge 20.7 km (7-9 hr); Day 3 Port Craig to Rarakau 20.2 km (5-7 hr). Ōkaka loop walk 45 min; Port Craig settlement and wharf side trip. Track text still says "Follow the South Coast Track over the historic Edwin Burn, Percy Burn and Sand Hill viaducts" (stale versus the April release).
- "South Coast Track is only accessible at low tide at Blowholes Beach. Watch for tidal surges at all times." Hazards: tidal surges and rogue waves, rockfalls, landslides, tsunami; wasps; hunting; "No mountain bikes or quad bikes"; no unauthorised drones; stay off helicopter landing zones.
- Fees: "See fee information on the Hump Ridge Trust website." Bookings: "Hump Ridge Track bookings are made through Hump Ridge Trust website. DOC hut passes cannot be used for these lodges." Tuatapere "1.15 hr from Invercargill Airport, 1.5 hr from Te Anau and 2.5 hr from Queenstown"; "Cars are left at the Rarakau car park at the owner's risk." Season dates: none stated. Contact 027 390 4520 or 0800 486 774, walk@humpridgetrack.co.nz, 31 Orawia Rd.

## DOC release 2 April 2026
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/news/media-releases/2026-media-releases/historic-viaducts-closed-for-safety-hump-ridge-track-remains-open/
- Percy Burn and Edwin Burn viaducts (Hump Ridge Track) and Francis Burn Viaduct (South Coast Track) "were closed in February due to structural deterioration". "The three-day Hump Ridge Track remains open, with hikers ... able to bypass the closed Percy Burn and Edwin Burn viaducts via an existing alternative track and bridge. A fourth viaduct, Sandhill Viaduct, remains open." 58 m swing bridge built bypassing Edwin Burn. No later DOC update found.
