# Lake Waikaremoana Great Walk 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21, lane max-k-walks)

## DOC Lake Waikaremoana Great Walk page (curl + text extraction; short URL https://www.doc.govt.nz/waikaremoana serves the same page)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/east-coast/places/te-urewera/things-to-do/tracks/lake-waikaremoana-great-walk/
- "46 km one way", "3 - 4 days", "Intermediate tramping track", "No dogs. Other pets on conservation land rules." "Bookings are open for the 2026/27 season."
- Quote: "There are four huts and five campsites on Lake Waikaremoana. These must be booked in advance. Camping on the track is only permitted at the designated campsites." Campsites named in the text: Waiopaoa (nearby the hut), Korokoro, Maraunui, Waiharuru (Hut and Campsite). That is four named; no fifth is named. The page says so as written; the guide says "five campsites but names four".
- Huts: Panekire (36 bunk beds), Waiopaoa (28), Marauiti (26); Waiharuru has NO facilities line with bunk count on the page. Each: heating, mattresses, toilets non-flush, water from tap not treated, boil before use. "Note: Whanganui Hut is closed". KiwiCamping hub data (huts/east-coast, asOf 2026-08-11) lists Waiharuru 40 bunks (largest in region) and Waiopaoa 30 (DOC page 28: only Waiharuru quoted on the page).
- Sections: Onepoto to Panekire 4-6 hr 8.8 km ("most strenuous part"); Panekire to Waiopaoa 3-4 hr 7.6 km; Waiopaoa to Korokoro 1 hr 30 min 3.6 km; Korokoro Falls 1 hr return; Korokoro to Maraunui 2 hr 30 min 6.8 km; Maraunui to Marauiti 30 min 1.7 km; Marauiti to Waiharuru 2 hr 6.2 km; Waiharuru to Hopuruahine Landing 4 hr 30 min 9.5 km. These sum to 44.2 km against the 46 km headline. The page also holds a stray "Approximately 1 hr 30 min - 2.1 km" line inside the last section (not used).
- "Walk 4-6 hours a day. Carry a pack of up to 15 kg. Climb and descend about 600 m over the Panekire Ridge." "Not recommended for children under age 10". Lake "between altitudes of 600 and 1200 m"; cold, snow, strong winds, heavy rain any time including summer.
- Day plan: 3 night/4 day: Onepoto to Panekire; Panekire to Waiopaoa; Waiopaoa to Marauiti; Marauiti to Hopuruahine. "Guided options are available."
- Facilities: "Huts ... don't have gas cooking facilities, lighting or toilet paper." "Some provisions are available at the Lake Waikaremoana Holiday Park store." "You cannot buy food on the track." "We do not provide emergency food in huts." Gear list: sleeping bag 3-4 season; "hut bunkrooms don't have heating and can be cold" (contradicts the hut facility line "heating"; the guide reports both). "Panekire: be prepared for low tank water levels at times during summer."
- Fees (all year): Adult NZ $35 / INT $35 hut; child NZ 5-17 $18, INT 0-17 $18; infant NZ 0-4 free. Campsite: $19 / $19; child $9 / $9; infant free. "There are no fees for entry into Te Urewera or to complete a day walk on the track." "Pay your fees by booking the huts and campsites before you start the track."
- Booking: "huts and campsites on the track all year round", transport booked separately; "Bookings are required for children and youth even though it's free for them to stay" (the page's own fee table lists $18/$9 for children; guide flags it); campsite booking needs group size and number of tent sites; penalty or turned away without booking. Water taxi and shuttles "available to both ends, but book early."
- Te Urewera: "Te Urewera is recognised in New Zealand law as a living person. Te Urewera is spoken for and governed by a board. Care for Te Urewera, including the tracks and facilities, is carried out by Te Uru Taumatua – Ngāi Tūhoe's operational entity." "Tūhoe wardens are stationed at major Great Walk huts during peak season." Visitors asked to respect "Tikanga of the Lake", Care for Nature (Manaakitanga Te Urewera) principles. No dogs or other domestic animals on the track "to protect ground-dwelling birds". Camping only in approved areas incl. lakeshore. No rubbish facilities. "Fires can be used for cooking or warmth, unless there is a temporary fire ban, however, a portable stove is a better option." Hunting by permit only.
- Getting there: SH38 from Waiotapu to Wairoa passes Te Karetu (visitor centre, Holiday Park). "gravel surface for about 90 km between Murupara ... and Tuai ... 4WD/SUV is recommended." Onepoto ~20 min from Te Karetu; Hopuruahine ~half an hour; "Hopuruahine road is not currently suitable for 2WD vehicles. This may impact access to Hopuruahine landing Great Walk-end car park" (2 km off main road). Parking: free up to 10 days lakeside towards the Holiday Park entrance; Onepoto and Hopuruahine car parks; visitor centre day-time only, "no multi-day parking"; "All parking is at the owners' risk".
- Water taxi (DOC): "Tūhoe offers a water taxi service to and from each end of the Great Walk, and to individual huts and campsites around the lake, out of Te Urewera Visitor Centre (Te Kura Whenua). ... usually available from October to April. It is not available during the winter season." Ngāi Tūhoe phone +64 6 837 3803. DOC names no operator.
- Alerts on page: Whanganui Hut closed; Hopuruahine road not suitable for 2WD.

## DOC Waikaremoana Holiday Park page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/east-coast/places/te-urewera/things-to-do/campsites/waikaremoana-holiday-park/
- "Serviced", "Bookings required", 26 non-powered/tent sites, 32 powered; chalets 10, lakeside cabins 12 + 1, tourist flats 2, family whare, bunkhouse. Shop (icecreams, coffee, snacks, ice), petrol and diesel, kitchen. "The DOC Campsite Pass cannot be used at this campsite." "No pets." 9.3 km from Onepoto, 25-40 minute drive, gravel. From Wairoa 57 km on SH38, one hour; from Rotorua 163 km, 3 hours on gravel (Te Urewera Rainforest Route). "The nearest town is Wairoa. It's one hour's drive and has supermarkets and other shops." Fuel also at Wairoa or Ruatahuna. "Mobile coverage is poor. Wi-Fi is available at the shop and the visitor centre." No fees stated on the page (see bookings page).
