# Heaphy Track 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21)

Slug `heaphy-track-camping-2026-27`, publishAt 2026-12-09. Lane t3-max-k-walks (CNTXT-876).

## DOC Heaphy Track page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/nelson-tasman/places/kahurangi-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/heaphy-track/ (curl, 200)
- "78.4 km one way", "4-6 days" on foot, mountain biking "2-3 days", Grade 4. "Mountain biking is only allowed from 1 May - 30 November." "No dogs."
- "The Heaphy Track has reopened as a through route with a new bridge across the Heaphy River." Image caption: "New swing bridge over Heaphy River".
- "There are 6 huts and 9 campsites, which must be booked well in advance all year round." The page names these campsites: Brown, Aorere Shelter, Perry Saddle, Gouland Downs, Saxon, James Mackay, Heaphy, Katipo Creek Shelter, Scotts Beach, Kohaihai: ten. Huts: Brown, Perry Saddle, Gouland Downs, Saxon, James Mackay, Heaphy.
- "Huts have: bunks. mattresses, heating, toilets, basic cooking facilities ... Huts do not have food, cooking utensils, showers or bedding." "Campers cannot use hut facilities." "Lewis Shelter is an emergency shelter - no overnight stays."
- Sections (walking time, distance; biking): Brown to Perry Saddle 5 hr, 17.5 km (2 hr 30 to 3 hr 30); Perry Saddle to Gouland Downs 2 hr, 7 km (1 to 2 hr); Gouland Downs to Saxon 1 hr 30, 5.4 km (1 to 2 hr); Saxon to James Mackay 3 hr, 11.8 km (2 to 3 hr); James Mackay to Heaphy Hut via Lewis Shelter 6 hr, 20.5 km (2.5 to 4.5 hr); Heaphy Hut to Kohaihai 5 hr, 16.2 km (3 to 4 hr). Sum 78.4 km. Flanagans Corner "At 915 m, this is the highest point on the track". "when the mist lowers, the featureless downs can be confusing and it is easy to become disorientated."
- Fees: huts 1 Oct to 30 Apr $44 / $66 / child $22 / $33; 1 May to 30 Jun $38; 1 Jul to 31 Aug $30; 1 to 30 Sep $38 (child half). Campsites 1 Oct to 30 Apr $19 / $28 / child $9 / $14; 1 May to 30 Sep $19 / $19 / $9 / $9. "There are no fees to complete a day walk on the track or for entry into the Kahurangi National Park." Matches Great Walks pricing page.
- Bookings: "Bookings are open for the 2026/27 season." "All huts and campsites on the Heaphy Track must be booked in advance all year round." Penalty or turned away if none.
- Access: "Brown Hut in Golden Bay - 156 km from Nelson - at the end of Aorere Valley Rd", "Kohaihai on the West Coast - 110 km from Westport - at the end of Kohaihai Rd". "Brown Hut is 156 km and a 2 hr 25 min drive from Nelson ... SH 60 from Nelson to Collingwood (128 km) ... up the Aorere Valley to Brown Hut (28 km)." "Kohaihai is 110 km and a 1 hr 45 min drive from Westport ... SH 67 from Westport to Karamea (95 km) ... north ... (15 km)."
- Transport: "Bus and taxi services are available to reach either end of the track from nearby towns. Regular bus services link Nelson and Westport. Vehicle relocation services are also available." "Air New Zealand flies into Nelson ... Sounds Air flies into Westport ... A number of local operators provide air transport into Takaka in Golden Bay and Karamea on the West Coast." "Some transport businesses may not operate in winter." "There is mobile phone coverage at both ends of the Heaphy Track. The landlines at Brown hut and Kohaihai campsite have both been removed." No operator is named on the page, so none is named on the guide.
- DOC brochure https://www.doc.govt.nz/Documents/parks-and-recreation/tracks-and-walks/nelson-marlborough/heaphy-track-brochure.pdf (pdftotext): "The ends of the track are 463 km apart by road, so you will need to arrange transport at each end. Bus, taxi, air and vehicle relocation services are available from both ends of the track. (Limited bus services operate in winter.)"; "The 148.8 m-long Heaphy River bridge is the largest suspended deck bridge built by the Department of Conservation."; "describes a 4-day hike for independent, non-guided walkers travelling from Brown Hut to Kohaihai"; "can be hiked in either direction".
- Bikes: "both directions", "no more than 6 riders", "half an hour before sunrise to half an hour after sunset" (Powelliphanta, roa), "Electric bikes are not allowed", no bikes in huts/shelters, "not a cycle trail", "advanced Grade 4", two full days for fit riders, 3 days 2 nights for less experienced, kea at James Mackay Hut and bike seats, track may close to bikes after storms or high tides.
- Hazards: rainfall "over 4,000 mm"; floods between Lewis Shelter and Heaphy Hut ("wait in either Lewis Shelter or Heaphy Hut"); lower Heaphy valley flooding "can reach 1.5 metres deep"; coast waves at high tide; Crayfish Stream "can flood quickly"; alternative track at Kohaihai River mouth; do not swim at Heaphy River mouth; tsunami risk "including Heaphy Hut"; giardia "small risk".

URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/nelson-tasman/places/kahurangi-national-park/things-to-do/campsites/kohaihai-campsite/ (curl, 200; the west-coast URL variant 404s)
