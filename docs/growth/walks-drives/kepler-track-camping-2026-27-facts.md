# Kepler Track 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21)

## DOC Kepler Track page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/kepler-track/ (curl, 200)
- "60 km loop", "3-4 days"; "Most walkers take 4 days/3 nights."
- Season "27 October 2026 - 30 April 2027". Outside: "1 May 2026 - 26 October 2026 ... Bookings required only for Luxmore Hut. Advanced and expert track categories."
- "There are three huts and two campsites along the Kepler Track, which must be booked well in advance during the Great Walks season. Camping or staying overnight in the shelters at Forest Burn and Hanging Valley is not permitted."
- Huts: "bunks, mattresses, heating, toilets, basic cooking facilities, solar-powered lighting, and cold running water. A DOC ranger is in residence. Huts do not have food, cooking utensils or showers." Campsites: "basic facilities including toilets, sinks and a water supply. Some have picnic tables and shelters. Campers can't use the hut facilities."
- Sections: car park to Brod Bay 5.6 km 1 hr 30; Brod Bay to Luxmore Hut 8.2 km 3 hr 30 to 4 hr 30; Luxmore to Iris Burn 14.6 km 5 to 6 hr; Iris Burn to Moturau 16.2 km 5 to 6 hr; Moturau to Rainbow Reach 6 km 1 hr 30 to 2 hr; Rainbow Reach to car park 9.5 km 2 hr 30 to 3 hr 30. "Luxmore Hut is a 45 minute walk from the bushline." Luxmore Cave 20 min return; Luxmore summit 30 min return, "highest point on the track (1472 m)". "Fill drink bottles at Luxmore Hut before leaving, as there are no reliable water sources along the alpine section until Iris Burn Hut."
- Bunks: Luxmore 54, Iris Burn 50, Moturau 40. Brod Bay and Iris Burn campsites: 15 non-powered/tent sites each, non-flush toilets, shelter for cooking, tap water not treated.
- Derived sums (not DOC text): Brod Bay to Iris Burn 8.2 + 14.6 = 22.8 km, 8 h 30 to 10 h 30; Iris Burn to car park 16.2 + 6 + 9.5 = 31.7 km, 9 h to 11 h 30.
- Fees: huts (Iris Burn, Moturau) 27 Oct to 30 Apr $88 / $132 / child $44 / $66; 1 May to 30 Jun and 1 Jul to 26 Oct $30 / $30 / $15. Luxmore $88 / $132 in season, $35 / $35 / $17.50 outside. Campsites 27 Oct to 30 Apr $28 / $42 / child $14 / $21; outside $10 / $10 / $5. "Proof of eligibility is required for the New Zealand rate." Matches DOC Great Walks pricing page.
- Bookings: in season "required for huts and campsites"; penalty or "turned away if the hut/campsite is full". Outside season: Luxmore Hut booked; "Iris Burn Hut and Campsite, Moturau Hut and Brod Bay Campsite: Bookings not required. Bunks are first come, first served." Hut payment "Backcountry Hut Tickets or a Backcountry Hut Pass"; campsite payment "Campsite Pass or Backcountry Hut Tickets".
- Access: "Kepler Track car park: 5 km from Te Anau. Fiordland National Park Visitor Centre: 50 min walk along the lakefront to the entrance of the track. Rainbow Reach car park: 12 km from Te Anau." "Shuttle buses provide a daily service to the entry and exit points on the track during the Great Walks season. A scheduled boat service also offers transport to Brod Bay." "You can book transport between Te Anau and the ends of the tracks, otherwise it's a 50 min walk." "Southland NZ has information about transport operators" (link https://southlandnz.com/plan-your-visit/transportation/). No parking rules and no operator names on the page.
- Avalanche: "The risk of avalanche can extend into December. During the Great Walks season, DOC manages avalanche risk and may close the alpine section between Luxmore Hut and Iris Burn Hut if there is avalanche hazard." Out of season "9 avalanche paths, and most have the potential to cross the track".
- Kea: "interested in your camping gear". Didymo: Lake Te Anau, Lake Manapouri, Waiau River, "currently not in the pristine Irisburn River". "not recommended for children under the age of 10". Rainfall "up to 9,000 mm per year".
- "The Kepler Challenge marathon event is held each year on the first Saturday in December and the Kepler Track will be busy with runners."
- DOC Brod Bay campsite page https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/campsites/brod-bay-campsite/ : walk-in Great Walk campsite, 15 sites; bookings required in season, not required out of season.
