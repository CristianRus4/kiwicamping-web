# Whanganui Journey camping 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21, lane max-k-walks)

## DOC Whanganui Journey page (WebFetch plus raw HTML)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/manawatu-whanganui/places/whanganui-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/whanganui-journey/
- 145 km Taumarunui to Pipiriki, "averaging 5 days"; 88 km Whakahoro to Pipiriki, "averaging 3 days", the most scenic; businesses offer 1-day and 2-day trips, some with jet boats. DOC's compare page rounds the short option to 87 km (great-walks-facts.md); the track page says 88.
- "Not a trip for beginners". Unguided: confident swimmer, canoe experience, 6-7 h paddling a day (8+ low flows), over 10, at least two in group plus supervising adults. Guided strongly recommended for less experienced and solo.
- Sections: Taumarunui (Ngā Huinga, Cherry Grove) to Ohinepane 22 km 3-5 h; Ohinepane to Whakahoro 35 km 6-8 h (Poukaria 14 km, Maharanui 17 km, Whakahoro 4 km); Whakahoro to John Coull Hut 37.5 km 7-9 h (Mangapapa 11, Ōhauora 16, John Coull 10.5); John Coull to Tīeke Kāinga 29 km 7-9 h (Mangawaiiti 9.5, Mangapurua 9, Tīeke 10.5); Tīeke to Pipiriki 21.5 km 4-6 h (Ngāporo 12.5, then 9 km). Total 145 km. Ōhura Falls side trip by short paddle up the Ōhura River.
- Huts: Whakahoro Bunkroom, John Coull Hut, Tīeke Kāinga (working marae; read tikanga). Alternative: Puketotara Hut about 1h30 up a track opposite Tīeke Kāinga. Campsites named: Ohinepane, Poukaria, Maharanui, Whakahoro, Mangapapa, Ōhauora, Mangawaiiti, Mangapurua, Ngāporo (9).
- Fees (per person per night, GST incl.): huts excl. bunkroom 1 Oct-30 Apr adult $35 (NZ or INT), child $18, NZ infant 0-4 free; 1 May-30 Sep $25, child $12.50. Whakahoro Bunkroom same in summer; 1 May-30 Sep $10 adult, $5 child. Campsites 1 Oct-30 Apr $19 adult, $9 child; 1 May-30 Sep $12, $6; Ohinepane and Whakahoro $10, $5 in winter. "There is no fee to enter Whanganui National Park" (page). Pricing page (https://www.doc.govt.nz/about-us/our-role/managing-conservation/recreation-management/great-walks-management/pricing/): summer huts $35/$35, campsite $19/$19 NZ and international, non-summer campsites $12 (not Ohinepane/Whakahoro). NOTE: the pricing page says the infant free rate listed applies to Lake Waikaremoana only, while the track page lists NZ infants free; the page cites the track page for infants.
- Payment: hut fees "Backcountry Hut Pass or Hut Tickets"; campsite fees cash in fee boxes at Ohinepane or Whakahoro. The page text does not say which applies in the Great Walks season; page says so.
- Booking: Great Walks season must book, fees paid at booking; outside, no bookings, first come first served. Penalty/turned away. "Not charged for an extra night caused by high river levels."
- Getting there: Taumarunui at Ngā Huinga (Cherry Grove) Domain; Ohinepane from Whanganui River Road (SH43) or river; Whakahoro turn off SH4 near Ōwhango onto Oio Road, about 43 km, "rough, narrow and winding"; Pipiriki about 76 km from Whanganui via Whanganui River Road or about 27 km from Raetihi on SH4 via Pipiriki Raetihi Road. Equipment hire, services, food and transport in towns near access points. No operators named.
- Safety: life jacket; do not paddle if flooding/rising; low flows harder; no cell reception on the river; distress beacon; no formal river grade given; Tarepōkiore whirlpool rapid, Ngāporo and Autapu rapids; Tamatea's Cave wāhi tapu; jet boat protocol; no alcohol; wasps Jan-May. Water untreated; toilets, no toilet paper; no food sold; no dogs, no open fires, camp only at designated campsites. Hire package: life jacket, kayak or Canadian canoe, paddles incl. spare, plastic drums, dry bags. "Denim, cotton and hiking boots are not suitable."
- Bridge to Nowhere: reached from Mangapurua Landing; 3 km, about 1h30 return, shared with cyclists; landing "very slippery when the rock is wet" and crowded with canoes and boats.
- No alerts or closures listed on the page. Page does not state season dates in text; the brochure and DOC compare page do (1 Oct).

## DOC Bridge to Nowhere walk page
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/manawatu-whanganui/places/whanganui-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/bridge-to-nowhere-walk/
- About 40 minutes from Mangapurua Landing; landing by jet boat up the river, or the Mangapurua Track from the Whakahoro or Ruatiti road end; "Fires are not permitted in the Mangapurua Valley"; treat or boil water; no closures listed; part of Te Araroa. Distance not stated. (Conflicts with the Journey page's 1h30 return; page tells the reader to check.)

## DOC Whanganui Journey brochure, October 2025 (PDF)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/parks-and-recreation/tracks-and-walks/wanganui/whanganui-journey-brochure-2025.pdf
- "Great Walks season: 1 October - 30 April"; must book in season, first come first served outside. Bunks: Whakahoro Bunkroom 10, John Coull Hut 24, Tīeke Kāinga 20. Map also marks campsites at John Coull Hut and Tīeke Kāinga. Per-campsite tent-site numbers on the map were NOT used: they could not be matched reliably (DOC's Ngāporo campsite page was reported as 18 tent sites elsewhere versus 32 on this map).
- Facilities in season: huts mattresses, water supply, toilets without toilet paper, handwashing, heating with fuel; may have lighting and basic cooking with fuel; hut ranger may be present. Campsites: toilets, sinks, water supply; some tables and cooking shelters; no rubbish bins. Outside season facilities greatly reduced, no hut rangers.
- "no exit points between Whakahoro and Pipiriki and you cannot turn back". Winter (May-Sep) only for very experienced paddlers. "Book your canoes, equipment and transport to and from the track." Contact: Tongariro National Park Visitor Centre, Whakapapa Village, SH48; phone 07 892 3729; tongarirovc@doc.govt.nz. Canoe photo credits to operators are not endorsements and were not used.

## DOC Whanganui Journey guide (PDF, undated)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/globalassets/documents/parks-and-recreation/tracks-and-walks/wanganui/whanganui-journey-guide.pdf
- Rain elsewhere in the large catchment can make the river rise several metres overnight; emergency communication by DOC radio only at John Coull Hut and Tīeke Kāinga; only road access at Ohinepane, Whakahoro and Pipiriki; no cellphone coverage at those three or on the river; no dogs, no hunting, no open fires.
