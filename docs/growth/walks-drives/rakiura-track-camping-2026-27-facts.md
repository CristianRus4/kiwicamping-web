# Rakiura Track camping 2026/27: facts (all read 2026-10-21, lane max-k-walks)

## DOC Rakiura Track page (read via WebFetch)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/southland/places/stewart-island-rakiura/rakiura-national-park/things-to-do/rakiura-track/
- Huts Port William and North Arm; campsites Maori Beach, Port William, North Arm. "Campsites are not next to the huts." Camping only at designated campsites; campers cannot use hut facilities. Each campsite: cooking shelter, water supply, toilet. North Arm campsite above North Arm Hut.
- "All huts and campsites ... must be booked in advance all year round"; bookings open for 2026/27; "maximum stay at each hut/campsite is 2 nights"; no valid booking: penalty or turned away if full. Carry booking confirmation and ID.
- Sections: Oban to Lee Bay 1h30 5 km; Lee Bay to Port William Hut 3-4 h 8 km; Port William to North Arm Hut 6 h 13 km; North Arm to Fern Gully car park 4-4h30 11 km; Fern Gully to Oban about 2 km road walk. Listed sections about 37 km + 2 km. DOC compare page (facts file great-walks-facts.md): 32 km, 3 days. The 2021 guide: "Distance: 32 km (loop)", day legs 8.1 / 13 / 11 km. Page uses both and explains the difference (5 km Oban to Lee Bay and 2 km road walk are outside the loop). The explanation is my arithmetic from DOC's own numbers.
- Campsite fees 1 Oct-30 Apr: NZ adult $19, intl adult $28, NZ child 5-17 $9, intl child 0-17 $14, NZ infants 0-4 free; 1 May-30 Sep $6 adult, $3 child. Huts: 1 Oct-30 Apr $44/$66; 1 May-30 Jun $38; 1 Jul-30 Sep $31. Matches pricing page (https://www.doc.govt.nz/about-us/our-role/managing-conservation/recreation-management/great-walks-management/pricing/): hut summer child NZ $22, intl $33; campsite non-summer adult $6 child $3.
- Mud: between Port William and North Arm huts long rain makes track "muddy above boot height, in patches up to 500 m long"; gaiters recommended; slippery netting and steps. Huts: no gas cooking, lighting or toilet paper; unheated. No food on track; no emergency food; rodent-proof containers. Mobile coverage limited to township; beacon recommended. Weather changes quickly, strong winds, hail, heavy rain any time. Kiwi mostly at night, red filter, no flash.
- Getting there: flight about 20 min from Invercargill; ferry about 1 hour from Bluff; no airline, operator or prices on the DOC page. Oban only township: accommodation, supplies, equipment hire, transport to track start. Shuttles can drop at Lee Bay; water taxis reach huts; North Arm boat access depends on tides; no operators named. Lee Bay is 5 km from DOC visitor centre via Main Road, Elgin Terrace past supermarket, coastal road via Horseshoe Bay. Fern Gully car park 2 km beyond visitor centre on Main Road.
- Visitor centre: 15 Main Road, Oban; phone +64 3 219 0009 (from DOC 2021 track guide). The page text read did not state opening hours, so the page gives none.

## DOC Rakiura Track guide, December 2021 (PDF, read with pdftotext)
URL: https://www.doc.govt.nz/documents/parks-and-recreation/tracks-and-walks/southland/rakiura-track-guide.pdf
- Duration 3 days, 32 km loop. Elevation profile: Maori Beach campsite 16 campers; Port William hut and campsite 24 bunks, 16 campers; North Arm hut and campsite 24 bunks, 16 campers. Older than the live page (2021): the page says "check the live DOC page".
- "No dogs or unpermitted drones allowed." Hunting year round in Rakiura National Park, "not permitted near the track". Two bridges between North Arm and Port William may become impassable in heavy rain. Maori Beach: "If the tide is low and conditions are safe, you can wade through the creek". Fern Gully to Oban 2 km (20-30 min). Walkers usually stay Port William and backtrack 40 min to turn-off.

## Southland District Council visitor levy
- https://www.southlanddc.govt.nz/community-and-facilities/working-living-or-visiting/stewart-island-visitor-levy/ : "All individuals travelling to Stewart Island/Rakiura, including freedom travellers, must pay the levy." Pay directly or "pay revenue to an Approved Operator". Exemptions include persons under 18 on arrival, visitors staying a continuous 21 days or more, and "Individuals whose visit is entirely within the Rakiura National Park". Page states no amounts or effective date.
- https://www.southlanddc.govt.nz/assets/Fees-and-charges/individual/Fees-and-charges-Stewart-Island_Rakiura-visitor-levy.pdf (SDC Schedule of Fees and Charges 2026-2027, pdftotext): column "1 October 2025": inbound $7.50, outbound $7.50 (passengers of approved operators), freedom travellers $15.00. The page says check how it applies to a track walker; this is not decided here.
