## Opua to Okiato vehicle ferry (Northland Ferries)
URL https://northlandferries.co.nz/bay-of-islands-ferry/ . Crossing time 10 minutes. Opua to Okiato first ferry 6:10am, last 10:00pm, departing from Opua Ferry Terminal, Beechy St. Okiato to Opua first 6:00am, last 09:50pm, departing from the wharf at the end of the Te Araroa Bypass, Okiato. "Departures every 10 minutes (approximately) between these hours." Shuttle "approximately every 10 minutes during the day, and every 20 minutes early morning and late evening. Opua is about 7 minutes' drive from Paihia and Okiato is about 10 minutes' drive from Russell." Suitable for "all vehicles including trucks, buses and campervans"; "low tides may dictate travel time for extra-large vehicles". Campervans "usually lower in the rear bumper. This can cause the bumper to scrape on the ferry ramp at low tides. To ensure travel without delay, please plan your Vehicle Ferry trip in the two hours before and after hight tide." Trailer boats and caravans advised the same four-hour range. Speed limit on ferry 5km/hr. Tickets bought on board (EFTPOS, VISA, MasterCard, American Express). No additional charge for passengers in the vehicle; prices one-way. Foot adult $2.70, child 5-14 $1.60, 0-4 free. Car/ute under 6 m $19.80; motorcycle $9.70; 2 berth campervan (<6m) $19.80; 2 berth toilet and shower campervan (6-8m) $33.80; 4-6 berth motorhomes (8-10m+) $41.10 to $81.70; vehicles 6-8 m $33.80, 8-10 m $41.10, 10+ m $81.70; car with caravan from $41.10 to $81.70; "Estimated cost only, vehicles are charged by length. Staff will confirm the price at boarding." Another page on the same site (Taking the car ferry to Russell, a blog post) says departures every 15 minutes; the ferry page figure (about every 10 minutes) is used and the page says to check with the operator.
