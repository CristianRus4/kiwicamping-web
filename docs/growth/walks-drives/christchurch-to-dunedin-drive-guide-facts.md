## Page arithmetic (labelled on the page)
- SH1 route Christchurch to Dunedin: 248 + 113 = 361 km; 3.5 + 1.5 = 5 hours (Tourism Waitaki). South Canterbury site gives 2h15m Christchurch to Timaru and 2h30m Timaru to Dunedin = 4h45m, so "about 5 hours".
- Inland alternative via Omarama and State Highway 83 to Oamaru: 345 + 118 = 463 km, 4 + 1.5 = 5.5 hours to Oamaru versus 248 km and 3.5 hours by State Highway 1: about 215 km and 2 hours more to reach Oamaru.

## Camping (DOC pages read 2026-10-21)

Sealed access."
