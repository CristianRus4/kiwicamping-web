## Page arithmetic (labelled as own arithmetic on the page)
- 382 + 261 = 643 km; 4.5 + 3.5 = 8 hours (museum figures). Love Taupo 3.5 + museum 1.5 + 3.5 = 8.5 hours. So "about 640 km, 8 to 8.5 hours".
- 382 - 262 = about 120 km Auckland to Hamilton (assumes same road). Labelled as arithmetic.

## Camping
- Love Taupo, Where can I camp. URL https://www.lovetaupo.com/en/stay/where-can-i-camp/ . "Certified self-contained vehicles can park overnight on any council-owned or council-managed land other than on our reserves and Ferry Road, where camping is prohibited." "You must also display your current self-containment warrant". Non-self-contained: three areas, Hipapatua Recreational Reserve (Reid's Farm) in Taupo, Mangakino Recreation Reserve, Whakamaru Recreation Reserve (no drinking water).
