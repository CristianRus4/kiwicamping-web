## DOC Milford Road/Milford Sound area
URL https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/places-to-go/milford-road-milford-sound-area/
- "The Milford Road (part of SH94) starts from Te Anau, and heads north towards Milford Sound/Piopiotahi (120 km)." "The nearest town with facilities is Te Anau, and there are limited facilities at Milford Sound/Piopiotahi." "Allow about 2 hours to drive the whole Milford Road, without stops."
- "Book Milford Road campsites online before your visit". Mobile reception: "Fibre internet has been installed along the Milford Road but mobile phone reception is limited. These Milford Road stopping points and campsites now have reception: Te Anau Downs, Totara Campsite, The Divide, Milford Sound".
- "Expect road closures and traffic delays due to weather conditions, rock falls, and snow/avalanche in winter." "Freedom camping (within 200 m of the road) is not allowed on the Milford Road."
- Also: half day walk on the Routeburn Track from The Divide to Key Summit; no drones without a permit.

## DOC Milford Road tips for drivers
URL https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/places-to-go/milford-road-milford-sound-area/milford-road-tips-for-drivers/
- Summer season October to April is busy; winter May to September has less traffic "but road conditions require more caution". Do not feed endangered kea.
- "Te Anau to Milford and back is 240 km (144 miles). An eight hour day is needed if you plan to take in the scenic sights and short walks along the way, and do a boat cruise". "Queenstown to Milford return is 600 km (360 miles) and takes 12-14 hours. We recommend that you plan your return trip to Milford from Te Anau."
- "There are no shops or fuel stations between Te Anau and Milford Sound. Public toilets are only available at Te Anau, Knob's Flat and at Milford." "Make sure you have a full tank of fuel before leaving Te Anau, and take food and beverages with you." "Dogs are not allowed in the national park."
- Homer Tunnel: "completed in 1953"; "at 945 m above sea level, is 1.2 km long and has a steep gradient down towards Milford. It's around 3.81 m tall."; "Traffic lights over summer peak periods control traffic flows through the tunnel. This can create delays of up to 20 minutes."
- Winter: "During winter (May to September), driving conditions can be extremely challenging. The road can often be covered in snow and ice." "advisable to leave later in the morning from 9 am onwards." "Check the current road conditions as snow chains may be required. Snow chains are available for rent in Te Anau. Make sure you know how to fit them". "Roadside information signs at Te Anau, Knobs Flat and Milford also advise current road conditions." "The section of road between the Hollyford Road junction and The Chasm is a Restricted No Stopping Zone as this is an avalanche area." "If you are not confident about driving in winter conditions, take a bus."
- Phones: card-phone at Knobs Flat, satellite phone at Homer Tunnel for emergency use only, card-phone at Milford Sound. Dump stations at Te Anau and Manapouri; "There are no dumping stations at any of the campsites on the Milford Road."

## Visit Fiordland
- Getting to Fiordland, https://www.fiordland.org.nz/plan/getting-to-fiordland/ : "Te Anau to Milford Sound 120kms (74.5 miles) Allow 2 hours driving"; "Queenstown to Te Anau 170kms (105.5 miles) Allow 2 hours driving"; "Access to Te Anau is via State Highway 6 and State Highway 94 from Queenstown"; "Driving from Queenstown to Te Anau ... along Lake Wakatipu, through rural Southland".
- Milford Road, https://www.fiordland.org.nz/visit/milford-road/ : SH94 "the only road access to Milford Sound Piopiotahi" (page text: "it is also the only road access to Milford Sound Piopiotahi"); "no shops or services along the way or in Milford Sound Piopiotahi itself"; "Drive time Te Anau to Milford with no stopping is a minimum of 2 hours"; "allow at least four hours ... if you want to stop"; "You need to allow 30 minutes to park and get transport or walk to the cruise terminal"; "Check in for your cruise is 20 minutes prior to your departure time"; "If you think you are going to miss your cruise you can usually be accommodated on a later cruise"; "During the winter months the road will be closed if there is avalanche danger until snow in the mountains can be displaced by explosives and the road cleared of debris by heavy machinery." The same page says "NO CELLPHONE COVERAGE along the Milford Highway" which conflicts with DOC's list; the guide uses DOC's wording. Page says 119 km in one place and 120 km in the FAQ; the guide uses DOC's 120 km.
- Driving in Fiordland, https://www.fiordland.org.nz/plan/driving-in-fiordland/ : "While the drive to Milford Sound can be done in two hours, it is a good idea to leave at least 3-4 hours"; "There are no passing lanes on the roads in Fiordland, if you have traffic behind you pull over and let it past"; check the Milford Road status on the NZTA website; "Keep left".

## Stops (DOC pages)
- Mirror Lakes Walk: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/mirror-lakes-walk/ : 400 m return, 10 min, wheelchair accessible, "about 56 km north from Te Anau".
- The Chasm Walk: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/the-chasm-walk/ : 400 m return, 20 min; "about 109 km along the Milford Road from Te Anau"; page describes February 2020 storm damage, reopening "on 24 April 2023 as a modified experience" and further bridge and car park work planned for 2023/24 and 2024 (page not updated since); check current status.
- Campsites (all https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/campsites/<name>/): Henry Creek 23 km north of Te Anau, 30 non-powered/tent sites, bookings required, no fires allowed; Walker Creek 47 km, 5 sites; Totara 50 km "south of Mirror Lakes", 20 sites; Deer Flat 62 km, 15 sites, toilets wheelchair accessible; Kiosk Creek (Knobs Flat) 63 km, 10 sites; Upper Eglinton 68 km, 3 sites, "Gravel road not suitable for larger vehicles"; Cascade Creek 71 km, 140 sites, "Your booking will be for a site, which will be allocated or selected on arrival". Every one: adult $18, child $9 (5 to 17), infant free; DOC Campsite Pass can be used; bookings required; no dogs. Henry Creek, Totara, Deer Flat, Walker Creek, Kiosk Creek, Upper Eglinton pages: "Bookings are open for stays up to 30 June 2027".
- DOC Fiordland National Park getting there https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/getting-there/ : Te Anau "can be reached by SH6 and SH94 from Queenstown"; DOC points to the AA calculator for times (no numbers given).
