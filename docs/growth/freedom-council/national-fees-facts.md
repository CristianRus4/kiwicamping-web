# National freedom camping infringement fees (council land)

Date read: 2026-10-21. Method: curl of legislation.govt.nz (open).

- Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023, Schedule 2, https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2023/0136/latest/whole.html
  - s 20(1)(a) camping in a local authority area in breach of a bylaw prohibition or restriction: $400 (max fine $1,200)
  - s 20(1)(b) using a motor vehicle that is not self-contained, other than where permitted: $400
  - s 20(1)(c) failing to display a warrant card, other than where permitted: $200
  - s 20(1)(d) self-contained vehicle with more people than certified for: $400
  - s 20(1)(e) damaging the area, flora, fauna or a structure, or depositing waste: $800 (max fine $2,400)
  - s 20(1)(f)-(h) making preparations to camp in breach: $400
  - s 20(1)(i) failing or refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer: $600
- Freedom Camping Act 2011 s 20E: infringement fee not exceeding $1,000 prescribed by regulations, or $400 if none prescribed; court fine up to $3,000 (https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2011/0061/latest/whole.html).
