# Whangarei District Council: freedom camping facts

- Council: Whangarei District Council (Whangarei Heads, Ruakaka, Waipu, Tutukaka/Matapouri, Ngunguru, Onerahi)
- Date read: 2026-10-21
- Method: plain curl for the council freedom camping page and the council-hosted bylaw PDF (pdftotext); WebFetch summary for the same page used only as a cross-check (one WebFetch figure, the notice date, is marked). No block met. Per-site PDFs and the GIS map were not read.

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** Camping in Public Places Bylaw 2017 (made under the Freedom Camping Act 2011). In force 21 October 2017; reviewed October 2020, amended 26 February 2021 (in force 1 October 2021); further amendments for the Self-contained Motor Vehicles Legislation Act 2023 in force 7 June 2025.
  - URL: https://www.wdc.govt.nz/files/assets/public/documents/council/bylaws/amended-camping-in-public-places-bylaw-2017.pdf (council page: https://www.wdc.govt.nz/Community/Parks-and-recreation/Freedom-camping). Read 2026-10-21.
  - Quote: "This Bylaw comes into force on 21 October 2017." and "It was reviewed by Council in October 2020 and subsequently amended on 26 February 2021." and "Subsequent amendments to align the Bylaw's provisions with amendments to the Act made by the Self-contained Motor Vehicles Legislation Act 2023 come into force on 7 June 2025."
- Next review date: not published in the text read.
- **Fact:** Applies to council land only.
  - Council page quote: "It does not apply to: Department of Conservation land / commercial campgrounds / privately owned public spaces (e.g. supermarket car parks)."

## 2. Self-contained vehicles
- **Fact:** Permitted across the district in any council local authority area except where prohibited/restricted, for self-contained vehicles and tents.
  - Quote (bylaw 6.1): "Camping using a tent or other temporary structure or a self-contained motor vehicle is permitted in any local authority area within the District, except for those areas where it is prohibited or restricted".
  - Quote (6.2): "Camping in a motor vehicle is restricted to self-contained motor vehicles only in all areas of the District, except for those areas listed in Schedules 3 and 4 of this Bylaw."
- **Fact:** Certification wording. Council page says only "Check if your vehicles complies with the rules set in freedom camping rule changes" and links MBIE. Bylaw: "A certificate may only be issued by a self-containment certification authority appointed by the Plumbers, Gasfitters, and Drainlayers Board... must display the warrant card". Blue warrants: not mentioned in the page or bylaw text read.
- **Fact:** In the 23 listed sites, self-contained vehicles are restricted to designated site or marked spaces (bylaw 8.1).

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact:** Non-self-contained vehicles only at 17 listed sites; tents at 5 listed sites.
  - Page quote: "If you are camping in a vehicle or a caravan that is not certified as self-contained, you may camp at the locations below." and "There are five areas you can camp in a tent on public land."
  - Non-self-contained sites: A H Reed Memorial Park (temporarily prohibited), Bascule carpark, Kowharewa Bay, Manaia Walkway carpark, Marsden Bay Reserve, Matapouri Wehiwehi Road, Ngunguru Library, Ocean Beach carpark, One Tree Point boat ramp carpark, Parua Bay, Reotahi, Sandy Bay, Tamaterau, Tarewa i-Site, Waipu Caves, Wellingtons Bay (Whangaumu Reserve), Woolleys Bay western carpark.
  - Tent sites: Parua Bay (about 15, 3 nights), Reotahi (up to 3, 1 night), Sandy Bay (about 4, 1 night), Tamaterau (page says 4 to 5 for tents, 2 to 3 for vehicles, 1 night), Waipu Caves (many, 1 night).

## 4. Nights, hours, numbers
- **Fact:** General rule: three nights in four weeks in any one area, and move 1 km.
  - Quote (bylaw 6.3): "no person may camp for more than three nights in any four-week consecutive period in any one area, or within 1 kilometre of any area they have previously camped in within that four-week period."
  - Page quote: "You can stay for a maximum of three nights in any other of Council's public places, except for the prohibited areas. Once you have reached the limit of your stay, you must move at least one kilometre away for your next stay and not return for at least four weeks".
- **Fact:** Designated sites: bylaw default is one night per four weeks unless the schedule says otherwise.
  - Quote (8.1(b)): "no person may camp for more than one night in any four-week consecutive period in any one area".
  - Note the page's table gives 3 nights only at Bascule carpark and Parua Bay; 1 night at all others.
- **Fact:** Only one vehicle per marked space; park legally; no emptying effluent tanks in public toilets.
  - Quote (6.4): "only one motor vehicle per marked space is allowed" and "no effluent tanks may be emptied in public toilets."
- Seasonal: Onerahi sportspark and Tikipunga sportspark: "Camping permitted from 18 December to Northland (or Auckland) Anniversary Day each year." (counts and nights not stated on the page).
- Arrival/departure times: not published. Group size: not published beyond site counts.
- Temporary closures by council up to 14 days (bylaw 12.3).

## 7. Fees and infringement
- **National (confirmed from legislation, read 2026-10-21):** Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023 (2023/136), in force 13 July 2023, made under s 43 Freedom Camping Act 2011. URL: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2023/0136/latest/LMS853112.html (PDF read: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2023/136/en/2023-06-12.pdf). Local authority areas (s 20(1)), fee / max fine:
  - (a) camping in breach of bylaw prohibition or restriction: $400 / $1,200
  - (b) using a motor vehicle that is not self-contained, other than where permitted: $400 / $1,200
  - (c) failing to display a warrant card: $200 / $600
  - (d) self-contained vehicle with more people than certified: $400 / $1,200
  - (e) interfering with or damaging the area, flora, fauna or structure, or depositing waste: $800 / $2,400
  - (f)-(h) making preparations to freedom camp (in breach, non-self-contained, over capacity): $400 / $1,200
  - (i) failing or refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer: $600 / $1,800
  - s 20C: altered or fraudulent warrant card or certificate, refusing or giving false information: $600 / $1,800.
  - The $800 fee is offence (e) only. Camping in a prohibited or restricted area or in a non-self-contained vehicle is $400.
- **Council:** Bylaw 14.1: "The penalties in section 20E of the Act apply to the offence of camping in breach of this Bylaw." Explanatory note: "The penalty is $400 or any other amount prescribed in regulations." Page: "Failing to comply with the rules can result in a fine under the Freedom Camping Act or the Reserves Act". No camping fee, permit or booking: "The page does not list any freedom camping fees" (not published). Prior consent (20 working days notice) available for waivers (bylaw 11).
