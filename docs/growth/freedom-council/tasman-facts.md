## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** Responsible Camping Bylaw 2025, adopted 11 September 2025, in force 1 November 2025. SEARCH EXTRACT (tasman.govt.nz/my-region/recreation/responsible-camping): "The Responsible Camping Bylaw 2025 was adopted on 11 September and came into effect on 1 November 2025." Draft title was "Freedom Camping Bylaw 2025"; draft says next review by 2030 (DRAFT).
- URLs: https://www.tasman.govt.nz/my-council/key-documents/more/bylaws-and-regulations/camping-bylaw ; https://www.tasman.govt.nz/my-region/recreation/responsible-camping (both 403 here; hand check).

## 2. Certified self-contained
- DRAFT cl 5.1: "A person must not freedom camp in any local authority area in the Tasman District in any vehicle that is not a certified self-contained vehicle, except as allowed in clause 6." DRAFT cl 4.1: freedom camping permitted in any local authority area unless prohibited or restricted. Self-contained: "valid certificate of self-containment issued in accordance with section 87U(3)(d)". Blue/green warrant: not mentioned in draft. Final wording: not read.
- DRAFT Schedule 2 general: "Freedom camping in a certified self-contained vehicle is restricted to the signposted area. Space will be on a 'first-in first-served' basis."

## 3. Tents / non-certified
- **Fact:** No tent freedom camping on public land. SEARCH EXTRACT of council page: "freedom camping in a tent on public land is not permitted in the Tasman District." DRAFT cl 5.2: "A person must not freedom camp in any tent or temporary structure in any local authority area, unless allowed for in clause 6."
- Non-certified vehicles allowed only at 3 sites (SEARCH EXTRACT of council page): Motueka Beach Reserve 3 spaces; Alexander Bluff Road Reserve, Ngatimoti max 40 vehicles; Fittal Street parking, Richmond 12 spaces. Matches DRAFT Schedule 2.

## 4. Nights, hours
- DRAFT cl 6.1.2: "the maximum period of stay permitted in any location specified in Schedule 2 is two consecutive nights on no more than two separate occasions in any calendar month"; location = land within 500m. DRAFT: vehicles must depart by 8am unless area-specific; contain possessions within one carparking space (or within 1m of vehicle where undefined); no fires. Decks Reserve: overnight 6pm-8am only on weeknights (DRAFT). Final may differ.

## 5. Prohibited

DRAFT only.

## 7. Fees
- DRAFT cl 9: penalties "specified in regulations" (s20E). Prior written consent from the Chief Executive possible, application at least 20 working days ahead (DRAFT cl 7). No camping fees stated.
**Fact (national regime, local authority land).** Infringement fees: $400 freedom camping in breach of a bylaw; $400 in a non-self-contained vehicle in breach of the national rule; $200 failing to display a warrant card (unless area permits non-self-contained vehicles); $800 interfering/damaging the area or depositing waste other than in a receptacle; $400 more people than the vehicle is certified for; $400 making preparations in breach; $600 failing/refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer. Max court fines are 3x the fee.
- URL: https://www.mbie.govt.NZ/assets/infringement-offences-table-for-website.pdf (MBIE table, "June 2023", in force 13 July 2023). Read 2026-10-21 (PDF text extracted).
- Quote: "From 13 July 2023, a person who commits an infringement offence is liable to the penalties set out in the table below." / "Freedom camping in breach of a bylaw $400 $1,200" / "Failing to display a warrant card ... $200 $600" / "deposits waste in or on the area ... $800 $2,400" / "Failing or refusing to leave a local authority area ... $600 $1,800".
