## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** Freedom Camping Bylaw 2021 (in force 1 Dec 2021), amended 15 Nov 2023 for the Self-contained Motor Vehicles Legislation Act 2023, changes in force 22 Nov 2023. SEARCH EXTRACT of https://ccc.govt.nz/the-council/plans-strategies-policies-and-bylaws/bylaws/freedom-camping-bylaw: "The bylaw was amended on 15 November 2023 in response to the requirements of the Self-contained Motor Vehicles Legislation Act 2023." ## 2. Certified self-contained
- **Fact:** Self-contained vehicles may camp in the City Coastal Restricted Zone and named sites. Newsline 2021 quote: "can freedom camp in the newly created City Coastal Restricted Zone". Blue/green warrant wording: not read.
- Newsline: "Up to 18 self-contained vehicles can stay there [Naval Point, Lyttelton], for up to two nights in any 30 day period." Akaroa: "there is an area set aside for freedom camping that can host up to 18 self-contained vehicles." (2021 text: verify current).
- City Coastal Restricted Zone, Newsline 2021: "the maximum stay in the Zone is now four nights in a 30 day period, with no more than two nights the same place." (2021; current unverified.)

## 5. Prohibited / restricted (SEARCH EXTRACT of council page)
- Central City: land bounded by Bealey, Fitzgerald, Moorhouse, Deans and Harper Avenues, and Little Hagley Park. New Brighton: council off-street car parks north and south of the pier. North Beach car park: restricted weekends and October to May (Newsline: "Banned at weekends, from October through to May, in the North Beach Carpark."). Southshore Spit: council land between eastern coast and Caspian Street. Takamatua hillside residential area (Banks Peninsula) banned (Newsline). Naval Point: all council land prohibited except the signposted area.

## 7. Fees
- CCC letstalk 20 Dec 2023 (https://letstalk.ccc.govt.nz/te-pataka-o-rakaihautu-banks-peninsula-community-board/freedom-camping-changes-and-activities-over-summer): Minister of Tourism "increased the fine for breaching a freedom camping bylaw from $200 to $400." Council adds no permits/fees (not read).
**Fact (national regime, local authority land).** Infringement fees: $400 freedom camping in breach of a bylaw; $400 in a non-self-contained vehicle in breach of the national rule; $200 failing to display a warrant card (unless area permits non-self-contained vehicles); $800 interfering/damaging the area or depositing waste other than in a receptacle; $400 more people than the vehicle is certified for; $400 making preparations in breach; $600 failing/refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer. Max court fines are 3x the fee.
- URL: https://www.mbie.govt.NZ/assets/infringement-offences-table-for-website.pdf (MBIE table, "June 2023", in force 13 July 2023). Read 2026-10-21 (PDF text extracted).
- Quote: "From 13 July 2023, a person who commits an infringement offence is liable to the penalties set out in the table below." / "Freedom camping in breach of a bylaw $400 $1,200" / "Failing to display a warrant card ... $200 $600" / "deposits waste in or on the area ... $800 $2,400" / "Failing or refusing to leave a local authority area ... $600 $1,800".
