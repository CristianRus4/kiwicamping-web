## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact**: Whakatane District Council Freedom Camping Bylaw, date made 17 Oct 2024, commencement 17 Oct 2024, next review 17 Oct 2029, made under Freedom Camping Act 2011. Standalone bylaw (previously in Public Places Bylaw, SEARCH EXTRACT).
- URL: https://www.whakatane.govt.nz/files/documents/freedom_camping_bylaw_2024.pdf ; page https://whakatane.govt.nz/node/122 (read 2026-10-21)
- Quote: "Date made: 17 October 2024 Commencement: 17 October 2024 ... Next review date: 17 October 2029"

## 2. Certified self-contained vehicles
- **Fact**: General permission for tents and self-contained vehicles on local authority land except prohibited areas (Sched 2) and Reserves Act limits; restricted areas (Sched 3) need certified self-contained, designated area only.
- Quote (cl 4.1): "Freedom camping using a tent or other temporary structure or a self-contained motor vehicle is permitted within a local authority area, unless it is restricted or prohibited in an area in accordance with: a. clause 5 or 6 ..."
- Sched 3 conditions: "1. Vehicles to camp only in the designated area. 2. Certified self-contained vehicles only. 3. Vehicles to camp for a maximum of three consecutive nights within any four-week period."
- Definition: "Self-contained vehicle has the same meaning as 'self-contained' in section 4 of the Freedom Camping Act 2011." Council page: "Vehicles must display a current self-containment certificate." Green/blue warrant wording: not published.
- Reserves Act note (bylaw): "Section 44 of the Reserves Act 1977 generally prohibits camping in reserves unless certain statutory exceptions apply." Page: "Freedom camping is also prohibited in all reserves under the Reserves Act 1977." (page text as extracted by WebFetch; bylaw says reserves listed in Scheds 1 and 3 are provided for in the Reserve Management Plan.)

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact**: Tents allowed under general permission (cl 4.1) outside prohibited areas and subject to the Reserves Act (so practically not in most reserves). Non-self-contained motor vehicles: only Schedule One site.
- Quote (cl 4.2): "Freedom camping using a motor vehicle that is not self-contained is only permitted in the local authority areas listed in Schedule One"
- Sched One: single site, "Aniwhenua Reserve, Galatea (near Murupara township)": "Maximum 7-night stay in any four-week period."; leave tidy; follow officer directions. (Council page also states this per search extract.)
- Note: Sched 3 restricted areas (Whakatane/Ohope) allow only certified self-contained vehicles, so tents/non-certified are not allowed at those four sites.

## 4. Nights, times, numbers
- Restricted areas (Sched 3): 3 consecutive nights in any one location within any four-week period. Quote on page: "A maximum stay of three consecutive nights in any one location within a four-week period."
- Seasonal: West End Road carpark: "Freedom camping is prohibited at West End Carpark from the beginning of the third week of December to 31 March."
- Aniwhenua Reserve: 7 nights per four weeks.
- Arrival/departure times, group size, per-site vehicle counts: not published (the 2013 counts in a search extract are outdated and not from the current bylaw).

## 5. Prohibited areas (Sched 2, read 2026-10-21; 14 entries, each with a map no.)
- Whakatane: Muriwai Drive (full length incl reserves, Fishing Club to road end); Kohi Point Scenic Reserve; Rex Morpeth Park.
- Ohope: Mahy Reserve; Wharekura East Reserve (177B Pohutukawa Ave); Ocean Road and Park, 243 Pohutukawa and Otao North Reserves ("along the length of Ocean Road"); Goodwins Landing; Uretara East and West; Otao South Reserve; Ohiwa Harbour Wharf car park.
- Otakiri: Braemar Springs (Wairere-a-tu).
- Thornton/Matata: Western Coastal Recreation Reserve, Otamarakau to Golf Links Road, about 30 km, "With the exception of No.2 car park"; Awatarariki Debris Flow Area (Clem Elliot Drive, Kaokaoroa Street, Richmond Drive car parks; high risk debris flows).
- Page also lists Edgecumbe Domain as prohibited (SEARCH EXTRACT only; not found in bylaw Sched 2 text read). Council page: "All prohibited and restricted areas are clearly signposted."
- Written permission can waive: apply 20 days ahead to Chief Executive (cl 8.1).

## 7. Fees and infringement
- National fees: skipped per instruction.
- Bylaw cl 11.1: "the amount prescribed by regulations made under section 43 of the Act as the infringement fee for the offence; or b. $400, if no fee is prescribed."
- Page: "Council can issue instant $400-$800 fines to anyone caught breaching the freedom camping bylaw." and "You may also be liable for prosecution."
- Site fees/permits/booking: none stated. Fee only for written-permission applications per Fees and Charges (cl 7.1). Homelessness: "Those experiencing homelessness are not subject to freedom camping infringements and penalties."
