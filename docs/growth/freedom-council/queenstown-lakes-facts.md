## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** QLDC Freedom Camping Bylaw 2025, in effect 1 December 2025 (replaces the 2021 bylaw, which the High Court quashed after a Motor Caravan Association judicial review). Council voted ~9-14 Oct 2025.
- URL: https://www.qldc.govt.nz/bylaws ; release https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/AK2511/S00773.htm (QLDC release 28 Nov 2025). Read 2026-10-21.
- Quote: "The Freedom Camping Bylaw 2025 takes effect on 1 December 2025" (paraphrase of release by fetch tool; date confirmed). lwb.co.nz (14 Oct 2025): "The High Court quashed the 2021 bylaw in the previous year" (secondary).
- Review date: not published (not read).

## 2. Certified self-contained vehicles
- **Fact:** Only certified self-contained vehicles, only in the 15 designated carparks (141 spaces), plus some rural roads under conditions. Urban areas prohibited.
- Quote (release, via scoop): "Freedom camping is prohibited in urban areas." Condition list: "Use a certified self-contained motor vehicle." (tool paraphrase; check exact wording on qldc.govt.nz/responsible-camping.)
- Rural roads (lwb.co.nz, secondary): "Park at least 5 metres from the road edge, on gravel or sealed surfaces." / "Stay at least 10 metres from rivers, lakes, streams, ponds, or wetlands."

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact:** Not stated in the release read. Most council reserves prohibited under Reserves Act. Treat as: tents/non-certified not permitted at designated sites (inferred from "certified self-contained" condition) - HAND CHECK on council page.
- Release: "Most Council reserves are prohibited for camping under the Reserves Act." Freedom camping allowed at Luggate Red Bridge Reserve subject to signage (release, tool paraphrase).
- LINZ land: SEARCH EXTRACT (web search summary) says not permitted anywhere on LINZ land in the district; not confirmed from a council page.

## 4. Nights, hours, group size
- **Fact:** Max two nights in any 30-day period; arrive after 6.00pm, leave before 8.00am (times vary at some sites); vehicle, equipment and personal items inside one marked space; no fires outside vehicle; remove all waste. Spaces first-come first-served, QR code check-in, availability dashboard (SEARCH EXTRACT for QR/dashboard).
- Quote (release via scoop): "Stay no more than two nights in any 30-day period, and do not return to the marked spaces within that period." / "Arrive after 6.00pm and leave before 8.00am." (tool-quoted).
- Seasonal rules: Beacon Point Road proposed summer restriction (see 10).

## 5. Prohibited / restricted
- **Fact:** All urban areas prohibited incl. on-road parking spaces; private land/roads not covered; Allenby Place carpark (Wanaka) temporarily prohibited (see 10). Named-place prohibition list: not read.
- Quote: "Freedom camping is prohibited in urban areas." (release).

## 7. Fees
- **Fact:** National fines apply; council release: "Breaching the bylaw or the Freedom Camping Act: starting at $400." and "Camping on QLDC reserves, or breaching conditions at Luggate Red Bridge Reserve: $800" (release via scoop, tool-summarised).
- No permits, booking or nightly fee: designated spaces are free (lwb.co.nz: "park for free in marked carparks").
**Fact (national regime, local authority land).** Infringement fees: $400 freedom camping in breach of a bylaw; $400 in a non-self-contained vehicle in breach of the national rule; $200 failing to display a warrant card (unless area permits non-self-contained vehicles); $800 interfering/damaging the area or depositing waste other than in a receptacle; $400 more people than the vehicle is certified for; $400 making preparations in breach; $600 failing/refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer. Max court fines are 3x the fee.
- URL: https://www.mbie.govt.NZ/assets/infringement-offences-table-for-website.pdf (MBIE table, "June 2023", in force 13 July 2023). Read 2026-10-21 (PDF text extracted).
- Quote: "From 13 July 2023, a person who commits an infringement offence is liable to the penalties set out in the table below." / "Freedom camping in breach of a bylaw $400 $1,200" / "Failing to display a warrant card ... $200 $600" / "deposits waste in or on the area ... $800 $2,400" / "Failing or refusing to leave a local authority area ... $600 $1,800".
- Not confirmed from legislation.govt.nz (returned 403 to WebFetch). Hand check: confirm Schedule 2 of Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023 (https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2023/0136/latest/LMS853112.html) is still current.
