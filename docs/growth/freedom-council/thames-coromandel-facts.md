## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** "Freedom Camping Bylaw/Te Ture a-rohe mo te Puni Mataraharaha ki ro waka", made 7 October 2022, commenced 5 December 2022.
- URL: https://www.tcdc.govt.nz/camping (also .../Our-Council/Bylaws-Codes-and-Policies/Bylaws/Freedom-Camping-Bylaw-2022). Read 2026-10-21.
- Quote: "This bylaw is due for review by 7 October 2027." Commencement date also confirmed by SunLive 7 Dec 2022 ("came into effect on Monday").

## 2. Certified self-contained
- **Fact:** Freedom camping only in a self-contained motor vehicle with a valid certificate of self-containment.
- Quote: "a valid certificate of self-containment issued in accordance with section 87U(3) (d)" (Plumbers, Gasfitters, and Drainlayers Act 2006); "a self-contained motor vehicle must be used to freedom camp." Page guide: "Freedom camp only in a certified self-contained vehicle." Blue/green warrant wording: not published on page (it cites Self-contained Motor Vehicles Legislation Act 2023 in force 7 June 2023, clause 14 revoked 8 Aug 2023).

## 3. Tents / non-certified
- **Fact:** Not permitted: bylaw clause 8(2)(a) requires a self-contained vehicle. Quote as above. Reserves: "Freedom camping on reserves is prohibited under section 44 of the Reserves Act 1977." (SearchExtract: SunLive says tents not allowed.)

## 4. Nights, hours, vehicles per site
- **Fact:** Quote: "a vehicle must not stay in an area more than two consecutive nights." / "freedom camping is only allowed between the hours of 5pm to 9am." Must leave by 9am. Some Schedule 2 sites stricter (below). Clause 8(4)(e): must not camp at a site with a vehicle limit "if that number of vehicles are already present when they arrive."
- Seasonal rules: none stated on page.

## 7. Fees
- **Fact:** Page gives no amounts: infringement fines "as prescribed by section 20E of the Act." No permits/fees/booking; approval to camp where restricted: application "at least 30 days before the freedom camping will occur."
**Fact (national regime, local authority land).** Infringement fees: $400 freedom camping in breach of a bylaw; $400 in a non-self-contained vehicle in breach of the national rule; $200 failing to display a warrant card (unless area permits non-self-contained vehicles); $800 interfering/damaging the area or depositing waste other than in a receptacle; $400 more people than the vehicle is certified for; $400 making preparations in breach; $600 failing/refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer. Max court fines are 3x the fee.
- URL: https://www.mbie.govt.NZ/assets/infringement-offences-table-for-website.pdf (MBIE table, "June 2023", in force 13 July 2023). Read 2026-10-21 (PDF text extracted).
- Quote: "From 13 July 2023, a person who commits an infringement offence is liable to the penalties set out in the table below." / "Freedom camping in breach of a bylaw $400 $1,200" / "Failing to display a warrant card ... $200 $600" / "deposits waste in or on the area ... $800 $2,400" / "Failing or refusing to leave a local authority area ... $600 $1,800".
