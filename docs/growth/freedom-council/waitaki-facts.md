# Waitaki District Council: freedom camping facts

Council: Waitaki District Council. Date read: 2026-10-21. Method: curl and WebFetch of council pages (not blocked), pdftotext of the bylaw PDF. No SEARCH EXTRACT facts except where marked.
Note: the council's own web pages and the bylaw disagree on the fee ($200 vs $400); see sections 7 and Hand checks.

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact**: Waitaki District Responsible Freedom Camping Bylaw 2021, in force 8 December 2021 (made 8 Dec 2021). Council web copy is titled "amended" in the PDF filename; the PDF text shows no amendment date.
- URL: https://www.waitaki.govt.nz/files/assets/public/v/5/files/our-council/policies-and-bylaws/bylaws/waitaki-district-responsible-freedom-camping-bylaw-amended.pdf ; council page https://www.waitaki.govt.nz/Facilities/Parks-and-Recreation/Freedom-camping/Freedom-camping-in-Waitaki . Read 2026-10-21.
- Quote: "This bylaw comes into force on 8 December 2021." Review date: not published in the text read.

## 2. Certified self-contained vehicles
- **Fact**: General permission on all council land except prohibited and restricted areas; certified self-contained vehicles only.
- URL: bylaw PDF cl 6.1, 6.2(a). Read 2026-10-21.
- Quote: "Freedom camping is permitted in any local authority area within the District unless it is prohibited or restricted" and "No person may freedom camp except in a certified self-contained vehicle."
- **Fact**: Vehicle wording on web page: "must be in a self-contained vehicle (with a toilet, wastewater tank and rubbish facilities on board) that clearly displays NZS5465:2001 certification." Bylaw: "Certified self-contained has the meaning set in the Freedom Camping Act 2011 or any subsequent legislation"; warrant "should be displayed inside the front window". Blue warrants: not mentioned (page/bylaw still cite NZS 5465:2001; not updated for the 2023 Act change).
- URL: council page above. Read 2026-10-21.

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact**: Not allowed on council land outside fee-paying campgrounds (no designated non-self-contained sites).
- Quote (bylaw 6.2(a)): "No person may freedom camp except in a certified self-contained vehicle." (freedom camp includes "a tent or other temporary structure"). Web: "If you don't have a self-contained campervan or motorhome with this certification you will need to find a campground". Enforcement page lists "Freedom camping in a non-certified self-contained vehicle or tent" as an infringement reason.
- Exempt fee-paying council campgrounds (cl 6.3): Waitaki Lakes campgrounds (Parsons Rock, Boat Harbour, Wildlife Reserve, Loch Laird, Sailors Cutting, Falstone Creek), Hampden Camping Ground, Omarama Campground, Kurow Camping Ground, Oamaru Top 10 Motor Camp, Harbour Tourist Park Oamaru, Dunback Domain camping area, Duntroon Domain camping area.

## 4. Nights and conditions
- **Fact**: Max three nights in any four-week consecutive period in any one area; four weeks start from first overnight stay.
- Quote (6.2(b)): "No person may freedom camp for more than three nights in any four-week consecutive period in any one area."
- Other conditions 6.2: effluent and solid waste to Council-approved facilities; legally parked; do not restrict public access; no washing dishes/clothes in public facilities. Arrival/departure times, group or vehicle counts: not published.
- Restricted area (Schedule 1): "Freedom camping in any Residential or Township Zones ... that is also within a 50km/hour zone can only occur, provided it is not within a direct line of sight from a residential dwelling outside of which the vehicle is parked, complies with the conditions in Clause 6.2 ... and the consent of the property owner or occupant has been obtained."
- Consent route: written application to the Chief Executive at least 20 working days ahead (cl 9.1, 9.2).

## 6. Site list
Not published as a counted list. Council states no designated freedom camping sites; permission is general. Only the fee-paying campground list in section 3.

## 7. Fees (council-specific)
- **Fact**: Bylaw cl 11.1: "every person who commits an offence pursuant to section 20(1) of the Act is liable to an infringement fine of $400."
- **Conflict**: council Enforcement page (read 2026-10-21): "we may fine you $200 on the spot"; Freedom-camping-in-Waitaki page: "may result in a $200 instant fine"; serious offences "up to $10,000" on prosecution. Treat the web figures as stale; national fees are in national-fees-facts.md.
- No permits fees, booking or paid freedom camping sites: not published. Consent applications (cl 9) fee: not published.

## 8. Enforcement and contact
- Phone 03 433 0300; email service@waitaki.govt.nz; Head Office 20 Thames Street, Oamaru. Council can check whether a place is council-managed by phone/email (FAQ). Ranger patrol statement: not published. Fines payable online (Visa/MasterCard), bank transfer or in person.
