# New Plymouth District Council: freedom camping facts

Council: New Plymouth District Council (NPDC). Date read: 2026-10-21. Method: curl of the council freedom camping page (page last updated 18 December 2025 per footer) and the bylaw PDF with pdftotext. Not blocked. Map images in the PDF were not readable.

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact**: New Plymouth District Council Freedom Camping Bylaw 2024, adopted 5 November 2024, in force 6 December 2024, next review by 5 November 2034.
- URL: https://www.npdc.govt.nz/leisure-and-culture/freedom-camping/ ; PDF https://www.npdc.govt.nz/media/loklku2f/ecm_9391100_v1_freedom-camping-bylaw-2024.pdf . Read 2026-10-21.
- Quote: "This Bylaw comes into force on 6 December 2024." and "next due for review ... by 5 November 2034."

## 2. Self-contained vehicles
- **Fact**: Permitted in any council area except prohibited areas and the two non-self-contained restricted areas; limited by certified people count.
- Quote (cl 9.1): "A person may freedom camp in a self-contained motor vehicle in any local authority area, excluding the non-self-contained restricted areas in Schedule 2, and the prohibited areas". Definition: "a motor vehicle that has a valid certificate of self-containment issued in accordance with section 87U(3)(d) of the Plumbers, Gasfitters, and Drainlayers Act 2006" plus transitional wording. Page: "your vehicle shows a self-containment certificate". Blue/green warrant wording: not used; bylaw cites the certificate and transitional periods (to 7 June 2025 or later Order in Council). Reserves Act note: "generally prohibits camping in reserves".

## 3. Tents and non-self-contained vehicles
- **Tents**: banned. Quote (cl 7.1(a)): "A person must not freedom camp ... in any local authority area in tents or other temporary structures". Page: "Camping in tents and other temporary structures on public land is banned in New Plymouth District."
- **Non-self-contained vehicles**: only at two restricted areas (Schedule 2): Lake Rotomanu, New Plymouth (six marked spaces) and Battiscombe Terrace, Waitara (six marked spaces). Quote: "Lake Rotomanu, New Plymouth - six marked spaces for non self-contained freedom camping. Battiscombe Terrace, Waitara - six marked spaces for freedom camping." First come first served.

## 4. Nights and conditions
- Self-contained: "a maximum of three nights in any 30-day period at any single location"; bylaw: "three periods of up to 24 hours in a 30 day period", same area = within 500 m (cl 9.1(b), 9.2).
- Non-self-contained: "a maximum of one night in a 30-day period", "one period of up to 24 hours in a 30 day period", within 500 m, marked spaces only (cl 8.1).
- Conditions: park legally; no fires; District Plan noise rules; do not prevent legitimate activities. Arrival/departure times: not published. Permits for dispensation: written, at least 20 working days ahead, decision 5 working days before (cl 10); permit fee not published.

## 5. Prohibited areas (Schedule 1 maps 1-14; page "Banned areas")
Fitzroy Beach carpark; Back Beach bottom carpark; Oakura beach front; Waiwhakaiho River Mouth; East End Beach; Kawaroa Park; Corbett Park; Battiscombe Terrace; parts of Tongaporutu; Fort St George; parts of Greenwood Road; Weld Road; Ahu Ahu Road; Motukari Reserve Carpark.
- Anomaly: Battiscombe Terrace is listed both as banned (Schedule 1 map 8) and as a restricted area for non-self-contained (Schedule 2 map 15). Document history says the 2020 amendment added prohibitions "excluding six restricted areas". Interpret as: prohibited except the six marked non-self-contained spaces; confirm on the maps.
- DOC: freedom camping at North Taranaki Visitor Centre car park prohibited from 19 December 2025 (DOC decision; page notes it).
