# Great Walks 2026/27: facts read from DOC pages on 2026-10-21

All from doc.govt.nz via WebFetch. Existing repo research (docs/growth/seasonal/nz-christmas-facts.md section 2) agrees.

## Compare page (https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/things-to-do/walking-and-tramping/great-walks/compare-great-walks/)
Lake Waikaremoana 46 km, 3-4 days; Tongariro Northern Circuit 43 km, 3-4 days (the 2026/27 route on its track page is 35.5 km); Whanganui Journey 87 or 145 km, 3-5 days; Abel Tasman Coast Track 60 km, 3-5 days; Heaphy 78.4 km, 2-6 days; Paparoa 55 km, 2-3 days; Routeburn 32 km, 2-4 days; Kepler 60 km, 3-4 days; Milford 53.5 km, 4 days; Hump Ridge 60 km, 3 days (booked via Hump Ridge Track Trust, not DOC); Rakiura 32 km, 3 days. That is 11 Great Walks listed; 10 are booked on DOC's system.

## Counts (track pages)
Abel Tasman: 4 huts (Anchorage, Bark Bay, Awaroa, Whariwharangi), 18 campsites; must be booked all year; max 2 nights per site 1 Oct-30 Apr, 5 nights 1 May-30 Sep; Totaranui Great Walk campsite one night all year; "There is strictly no freedom camping."; hammocks not permitted; Awaroa tidal crossing 1h30 before to 2h after low tide, daylight only.
Heaphy: 6 huts, page says 9 campsites but names 10 (Brown, Aorere Shelter, Perry Saddle, Gouland Downs, Saxon, James Mackay, Heaphy, Katipo Creek Shelter, Scotts Beach, Kohaihai). Booked all year. Bikes 1 May-30 Nov.
Kepler: 3 huts (Luxmore, Iris Burn, Moturau), 2 campsites (Brod Bay, Iris Burn); season 27 Oct 2026-30 Apr 2027.
Routeburn: 3 huts, 2 campsites; season 1 Nov 2026-30 Apr 2027; huts Routeburn Falls, Lake Mackenzie (Lake Howden destroyed Feb 2020; Routeburn Flats hut and campsite named on the page).
Milford: 3 huts (Clinton, Mintaro, Dumpling), no camping; season 1 Nov 2026-30 Apr 2027, last departure 28 Apr; one direction only.
Paparoa: 3 huts (Ces Clark, Moonlight Tops, Pororari), no campsites.
Rakiura: 2 huts (Port William, North Arm), 3 campsites (Maori Beach, Port William, North Arm); max 2 nights each; booked all year; no season window stated.
Lake Waikaremoana: 4 huts (Panekire, Waiopaoa, Marauiti, Waiharuru; Whanganui Hut closed), "five campsites" (4 named); booked all year; no dogs.
Whanganui Journey: 145 km Taumarunui-Pipiriki or 88 km Whakahoro-Pipiriki; booked in Great Walks season (from 1 Oct).
Tongariro Northern Circuit: 3 huts (Mangatepopo, Oturere, Waihohonu) each with a campsite; season 23 Oct 2026-30 Apr 2027; camping not allowed within 500 m of the track; bookings required all year. DOC release 4 Sep 2026: loop runs this summer once Mangatepopo Track reopens mid-December; Oturere Hut and campsite open for bookings from 18 December 2026 (hut delayed; DOC offering change or full refund). Track page (older wording): 2026/27 route starts Mangatepopo Carpark, ends Whakapapa Village.

## Booking
2026/27 openings at 9:30am, stays 1 July 2026-30 June 2027: Paparoa 12 May, Milford 13 May, Abel Tasman 14 May, Routeburn 15 May, Kepler 19 May, Heaphy, Whanganui, Waikaremoana 20 May, TNC and Rakiura 21 May. Hump Ridge booked separately, up to two years ahead. DOC release says nothing about the 2027/28 opening dates (read 2026-10-21): "not published".
Great Walks T&Cs (https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/bookings-terms-and-conditions/great-walks-terms-and-conditions/): cancellation fee 0% on the day you book (and the day after if more than 2 days before the stay), 10% from 48 hours after booking to 15 days before arrival, 50% 14 to 4 days before, 75% 3 days to the day before, 100% day before/day of/after arrival (page's wording: "Day before, day of, or after arrival: 100%"). Transfers only within the current booking season and not to a different hut or campsite. Check-in 2pm to 8.30pm, check-out 10am. Resident rate needs proof (passport, birth certificate, NZ driver licence, or a utility invoice); if not shown, booking invalid and rate difference plus $50 per person. Penalty without booking: normal age fee plus full adult fee.
Campsite Pass cannot be used at Great Walk campsites (docs/growth/seasonal/nz-christmas-facts.md 1.6).
