# Waikato District Council: freedom camping facts

- Council: Waikato District Council (Raglan, Port Waikato, Huntly, Ngaaruawaahia, Tuakau, Te Kauwhata, Onewhero)
- Date read: 2026-10-21
- Method: plain curl and WebFetch of council pages; bylaw PDF downloaded by curl and read with pdftotext (text readable). Council site was NOT blocked. Schedule maps (bylaw pp 12-43 and per-site map PDFs) are images with no text: not read.

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact**: Waikato District Council Freedom Camping Bylaw 2023, made 20 Nov 2023, in force 20 Nov 2023, amended 22 Dec 2025 (James Street), next review due 20 Nov 2028.
- URL: https://www.waikatodistrict.govt.nz/docs/default-source/your-council/plans-policies-and-bylaws/bylaws/freedom-camping-bylaw.pdf ; page https://www.waikatodistrict.govt.nz/camping (read 2026-10-21)
- Quote: "The bylaw shall come into force on 20 November 2023." / table row "Bylaw amended 22 December 2025 WDC2512/4" / "The bylaw is due for review by 20 November 2028."
- Note: the Oct 2025 consultation notice calls it the "Freedom Camping Bylaw 2022" (SEARCH EXTRACT); the bylaw PDF itself says 2023.

## 2. Certified self-contained vehicles
- **Fact**: General permission district-wide except prohibited areas; self-contained only (certificate), 3 nights.
- URL: https://www.waikatodistrict.govt.nz/camping (read 2026-10-21)
- Quote: "If you are in a self-contained vehicle you can camp anywhere in our district except in the prohibited areas. You can stay up to 3 nights in each area."
- Bylaw cl 6.2(a) (read 2026-10-21): "A person must not Freedom Camp unless they are Freedom Camping in a vehicle that is Self-contained"
- Definition (bylaw, quoting the Act): "the vehicle has a valid certificate of self-containment" ; "the standard requires that a self-contained vehicle be equipped with a toilet that is permanently fixed to the vehicle."
- Green warrant / blue warrant wording: not published (no mention of blue warrants).

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact**: Not allowed on council land generally (6.2(a)). Exception: only Onewhero Domain and Te Kauwhata Domain, max 2 nights, designated area only, free. Page does not mention tents; bylaw Schedule 3 covers "vehicles that are not Self-contained" only.
- URL: camping page (read 2026-10-21)
- Quote: "You can camp at Onewhero Domain and Te Kauwhata Domain but you can't camp in these areas for more than 2 nights if you aren't self-contained. It is free to camp in these areas." / "Otherwise you'll need to stay in a holiday park, campground or a Department of Conservation camping area."
- Bylaw Sched 3 (read 2026-10-21): "Onewhero Domain 14 Hall Road: Location is restricted to a designated area. The designated area cannot be used if it is required for events." Same for "Te Kauwhata Domain Mahi Road".
- Tents: not published beyond the above.

## 4. Nights, times, numbers
- **Fact**: 3 consecutive nights in any one location in any one month; may not camp within 500 m of a place already camped 3 nights in the month.
- Quote (bylaw cl 6.2 b, c): "must not Freedom Camp in any one location for more than three consecutive nights in any one month period." / "must not Freedom Camp within 500 metres of a location in which they have already been freedom camping..."
- St Stephens carpark, Tuakau (8 St Stephens Avenue): "Sign posted part of car park only. Permitted camping hours between 6pm and 10am. Maximum stay of two nights."
- Vehicle-count clause exists (cl 6.5 d: "maximum number of vehicles in a designated location") but no counts are stated in the schedules. Counts: not published in readable text (maps unread).
- Seasonal rules: not published. Group size: not published.

## 7. Fees and infringement
- National fees: skipped per instruction (see national-fees-facts.md).
- Council: "Under the Waikato District Council Freedom Camping Bylaw you can receive a $400 fine if you do not comply with the rules. You can also be fined for littering or making too much noise." (camping page, read 2026-10-21)
- Camping at Onewhero and Te Kauwhata: free. No permits or booking for general camping. Consent to camp in a prohibited/restricted area: written application at least 30 days ahead with fee per council fees and charges (bylaw cl 7.2).

## 10. 2025-2026 changes
- **James Street Raglan**: ban from 22 Dec 2025. URL https://www.waikatodistrict.govt.nz/news/article/2025/12/18/freedom-camping-ban-for-james-street-raglan (read 2026-10-21). Quote: "Freedom camping will not be allowed anywhere along James Street, Raglan, from 22 December 2025." Consultation 13 Oct to 14 Nov 2025, 165 submissions (74 supported, 26 in part), hearing 3 Dec. Reasons: alcohol, loud music, public urination, overcrowding, washing lines and cooking on berms, pressure on facilities. Article: "there are still plenty of spots where you can camp". Fines in article: not mentioned.
- 2023 bylaw change: nights increased from 3 vs proposed 5; SEARCH EXTRACT says final is 3 nights (matches bylaw text).
