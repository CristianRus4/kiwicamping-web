## 2. Self-contained vehicles
- **Fact (council page, current):** Quote: "Freedom Camping is only permitted if your vehicle is certified self-contained." and "Please note that Council reserves are not permitted for freedom camping (under the Reserves Act) and fines may be issued for rule breakers."
- **Fact (2018 bylaw, if still in force):** Freedom camping permitted in any local authority area unless prohibited; entire district restricted to certified self-contained vehicles.
  - Quote (cl 6): "Freedom camping is permitted in any local authority area within the Westland District unless it is restricted or prohibited". (cl 8(1)(a)): "Freedom camping is limited to 'Certified Self-Contained Vehicles' in accordance with NZS 5465:2001". Non-self-contained "is prohibited on all land owned and administered by the local authority" except "Responsible Camping Sites".
- **Fact (2024 proposal):** Self-contained vehicles or tents "permitted in any local authority area within the District unless it is prohibited or restricted" (cl 5.1), but cl 6.2: "Freedom camping in a tent or other temporary structure on any local authority area in the district is prohibited." and 6.3 "within 20 meters of a lake or seashore or beach frontage is prohibited unless permitted in Schedule 2".
- Blue warrant wording: not mentioned on the page or in the bylaws read (2024 draft only refers to the Act's transitional period: "Transitional period ... 7 June 2025").

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact (council page, current):** Quote: "If your vehicle is not certified self-contained or you wish to camp in a tent please stay with one of our commercial operators."
- 2024 draft: non-self-contained vehicles allowed only at the Schedule 2 sites marked "Vehicles that are not self-contained may be used" (Kumara, Hokitika Sunset Point and beachfront, Woodstock, Franz Josef Heliport, Paringa, Haast). 2018: only at the 8 "Responsible Camping Sites" (below).

## 4. Nights, hours, numbers
- **2024 draft cl 7.1(b):** Quote: "The maximum stay in any one location is two nights within a 30-day-period and the location must not be within 500 meters of a location where the person has previously freedom camped within a 30-day period". Hours 8pm to 7am at the Schedule 2 sites; "Parking available: Until allocated carpark is full". No fires (cl 7.1(g)). Wastewater to dump sites (below).
- **2018 bylaw:** Quote (cl 8(1)(b)): "no more than two consecutive nights, and no more than 10 nights per 30-day period". Responsible Camping Sites: 2 consecutive nights, 10 nights per calendar month, free of charge.
- Arrival/departure times in 2018: not published.

## 7. Fees and infringement
- **National (confirmed from legislation, read 2026-10-21):** Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023 (2023/136), in force 13 July 2023, made under s 43 Freedom Camping Act 2011. URL: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2023/0136/latest/LMS853112.html (PDF read: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2023/136/en/2023-06-12.pdf). Local authority areas (s 20(1)), fee / max fine:
  - (a) camping in breach of bylaw prohibition or restriction: $400 / $1,200
  - (b) using a motor vehicle that is not self-contained, other than where permitted: $400 / $1,200
  - (c) failing to display a warrant card: $200 / $600
  - (d) self-contained vehicle with more people than certified: $400 / $1,200
  - (e) interfering with or damaging the area, flora, fauna or structure, or depositing waste: $800 / $2,400
  - (f)-(h) making preparations to freedom camp (in breach, non-self-contained, over capacity): $400 / $1,200
  - (i) failing or refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer: $600 / $1,800
  - s 20C: altered or fraudulent warrant card or certificate, refusing or giving false information: $600 / $1,800.
  - The $800 fee is offence (e) only. Camping in a prohibited or restricted area or in a non-self-contained vehicle is $400.
- **Council:** 2018 bylaw penalty clause: "liable to a fee of $200 for each offence" (superseded by the 2023 regulations: $400). 2024 draft: cl 12.1 "infringement fine specified in regulations made under the Act, or ... $400". Site donation (Kumara, Woodstock): amount not published. Prior written consent for prohibited/restricted areas: apply to the Chief Executive at least two weeks ahead (2024 draft cl 9.2; 2018 same).
