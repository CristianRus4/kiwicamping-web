# Buller District Council: freedom camping facts

Council: Buller District Council. Date read: 2026-10-21. Method: WebFetch and curl of the council page and the bylaw PDF (not blocked); pdftotext of the PDF.
Note: the bylaw text is the 2012 bylaw as amended 2018, and is out of date against the 2023 Act.

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact**: Buller District Council Freedom Camping Bylaw 2012, in force 30 August 2012; amendments in force 29 November 2018 (resolution 28 November 2018).
- URL: https://bullerdc.govt.nz/recreation/freedom-camping/ and https://bullerdc.govt.nz/media/vrifsbba/181119-bdc-freedom-camping-bylaw-2012.pdf . Read 2026-10-21.
- Quote: "This Bylaw came into force on 30 August 2012. Amendments to this Bylaw came into force on 29 November 2018." Review date: not published.

## 2. Self-contained vehicles
- **Fact**: May camp anywhere on council land outside "no freedom camping zones" if self-contained; bylaw definition is NZS 5465:2001, three-day capacity.
- Quote (cl 6(1)): "A person may freedom camp in a self-contained vehicle in any local authority outside of any no camping zone as defined in Schedule A". Definition: "capability of meeting the ablutionary and sanitary needs of occupants of that vehicle for a minimum of three days ... and complies with New Zealand Standard 5465:2001". Web page: only "certified self-contained camper" may freedom camp. Blue/green warrants: not mentioned.

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact**: Not provided for. Bylaw has no permitted zone for tents or non-self-contained vehicles. Page: "Other vehicles should" use "a campground with facilities". (Bylaw defines freedom camping to include tents, caravans and non-self-contained vehicles but only permits self-contained.) Consent possible in writing to the CEO (cl 7).

## 4. Nights
- **Bylaw**: "limited to no more than three consecutive days, and no more than 10 days per calendar month", at any one location or within 500 m (cl 6(1)(a)). No fires (cl 8).
- **Web page**: encourages "no more than two consecutive nights in one place" and "a maximum of nine nights per calendar month". These differ from the bylaw; report both.
- Arrival/departure times, group size: not published.

## 5. Prohibited areas (Schedule A, no camping without prior written consent)
(a) Oparara River mouth west of Kohaihai Road, Karamea; (b) Flagstaff, Karamea; (c) beach access tracks at the Dolphin Reserve, Hector (north of Ngakawau River); (d) both Buller River Tipheads, Westport; (e) all of Marrs Beach east of Tiphead Road; (f) parking areas at the eastern end of the Buller Bridge, Westport; (g) Carters Beach Domain from Golf Links Road to the Top 10 Holiday Park; (h) Tui Dellaca Reserve and the grass area south of Tauranga Beach Road, Tauranga Bay; (i) Okari Road from the causeway to the Okari River lagoon; (j) first layby north of Nile River mouth, Charleston; (k) end of Hands Road, Charleston; (l) Constant Bay; (m) Joyce Bay; (n) all areas from Fox River market entrance south to the Punakaiki River boundary (excluding SH corridor); (o) Bridge Street (Broadway to The Strand) and The Strand to Reefton Swimming Pool carpark, Reefton; (p) all cemeteries in the district.
- Council web page list also names "Kohaihai Road, Karamea" and "Omau Domain, Cape Foulwind", which are not in the 2018 Schedule A text read (web page differs from PDF; flag).
- Karamea, Westport, Charleston, Reefton: no designated sites.
