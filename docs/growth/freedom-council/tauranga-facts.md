- Date read: 2026-10-21
- Method: plain curl and WebFetch on council pages and the council-hosted bylaw PDF (pdftotext), WebSearch for change check. No block met on the pages below. The bylaw map pages (Schedule 3, 10 maps) are drawings: not read visually.

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** Tauranga Freedom Camping Bylaw 2019 (titled "Amended Freedom Camping Bylaw 2019"): first adopted 2013, revised 2019 and 2023, in force 1 July 2019, review date 2028.
  - URL: https://www.tauranga.govt.nz/Portals/0/data/council/bylaws/files/freedom_camping_bylaw.pdf (council page: https://www.tauranga.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/travel-and-tourism/freedom-camping). Read 2026-10-21.
  - Quote: "This Bylaw comes into force on 1 July 2019." and "Revisions/Amendments 2019, 2023" and "Review date 2028".
- **Fact:** Applies only to council land.
  - Quote: "The provisions of this Bylaw only apply to land owned and controlled by Council, it does not apply to land owned by other organisations, for example the Department of Conservation, New Zealand Transport Agency, and Land Information New Zealand."

## 2. Self-contained vehicles
- **Fact:** Permitted only in listed reserves, roads and car parks (Schedule 1), not generally.
  - Quote (bylaw cl 6.1): "Freedom Camping is permitted in the Local Authority Areas set out in schedule 1 subject to the following general restrictions and conditions" and "(a) must be in a Self-Contained Vehicle".
  - Council page quote: "There are a number of reserves, roads and car parks in Tauranga where self-contained mobile homes can freedom camp."
- **Fact:** Certification wording on council page. Blue warrants: not mentioned on the page or in the bylaw text read.
  - Quote: "you must be in a certified self-contained vehicle that complies with the Self-contained Motor Vehicles Legislation Act 2023. If you don't have a self-contained vehicle with the necessary certification, you will need to find a campground".
  - Bylaw definition: "has a valid certificate of self-containment issued in accordance with section 87U(3)(d) of the Plumbers, Gasfitters, and Drainlayers Act 2006 (but see subpart 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 1AA for the meaning of self-contained during the transitional period)".
- **Fact:** Roads with limit of 50 km/h or less allowed with conditions; vehicles under 3.5 tonnes gross in residential areas and some car parks.
  - Quote (Schedule 1.2): "Must be parked within the confines of a formed and marked Parking Space." / "Not parked directly outside a residence, commercial or industrial premises on the same side of the Road." / "Must be less than 3.5 tonnes gross vehicle mass in a residential area."
  - Car parks named as allowed (Schedule 1.3): The Strand Waterfront, Dive Crescent, Masonic, Devonport Road, Eleventh Avenue.

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact:** Not allowed. Council page names no tent option; directs to campgrounds.
  - Quote: "If you don't have a self-contained vehicle with the necessary certification, you will need to find a campground". Tents: not published on the page (bylaw cl 6.1(a) requires a self-contained vehicle).

## 4. Nights, hours, numbers
- **Fact:** Two nights per location per calendar month.
  - Quote (council page): "You can only stay in any one location for two nights per calendar month, however you can visit other permitted locations."
  - Quote (bylaw 6.1(f)): "the maximum period of stay in any one Park, Reserve or Council car park or in any one Street is two nights per calendar month".
- **Fact:** Must stay in the designated area; no parking nearby if it is closed.
  - Quote: "If the designated area is inaccessible for whatever reason, you may not park nearby to freedom camp."
- **Fact:** Five reserves allow camping only 7pm Sunday to 10am Friday (Fergusson, Gordon Spratt, Greerton, Pemberton, Waipuna). Others "Available at all times". Gate-lock hours vary by summer and winter for several reserves (see council page table).
  - Quote: "7pm Sunday - 10am Friday only".
- **Fact:** Mount Greens car park rule.
  - Quote: "Must fit within the confines of a marked and formed parking space from 7am to 8pm. Between the hours of 8pm to 7am vehicles must be parked within the area marked as car parking spaces".
- Arrival/departure times otherwise, group size: not published.

## 5. Prohibited areas (bylaw Schedule 2)
- **Fact:** Parks and reserves prohibited include Anzac Park, Arataki Park, Bellevue Park, Blake Park, Dive Crescent Reserve, Gordon Carmichael Reserve, Gate Pa Domain, Kopurererua Valley, Links Avenue Reserve, Macville Park, Matua Park, May Street Reserve, Morland Fox Park, Oceandowns Reserve, Omanu Beach Reserve (surf club car park), Papamoa Domain, 123 Maunganui Road reserve, Selwyn Park, Sulphur Point, Sydenham Botanic Park, Tauranga and Wharepai Domains, Yatton Park, plus drainage reserves, third-party leased halls, cemeteries, reserves without formed car parks.
  - Quote: "Freedom Camping is prohibited in the areas identified in schedule 2 of this Bylaw." (cl 7.1)
- **Fact:** Roads and car parks prohibited: all roads over 50 km/h; The Mall, Pilot Bay and Marine Parade (Mount Maunganui); Turret Road; all service lanes; City Council Administration car park; May Street, Cliff Road, Greerton Road car parks.
  - Council page also lists: "Maunganui Road car park", "Shadelands Lane", "The foreshore side of Harbour Drive", "Omanu Surf Club car park". Note the page and bylaw lists differ slightly: page is an "Some places you can't park overnight are" list.

## 7. Fees and infringement
- **National (confirmed from legislation, read 2026-10-21):** Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023 (2023/136), in force 13 July 2023. URL: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2023/0136/latest/LMS853112.html (and the PDF https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2023/136/en/2023-06-12.pdf). Local authority areas (s 20(1)), fee / max fine:
  - (a) camping in breach of bylaw prohibition or restriction: $400 / $1,200
  - (b) using a motor vehicle that is not self-contained, other than where permitted: $400 / $1,200
  - (c) failing to display a warrant card: $200 / $600
  - (d) self-contained vehicle with more people than certified: $400 / $1,200
  - (e) interfering with or damaging the area, flora, fauna or structure, or depositing waste: $800 / $2,400
  - (f)-(h) making preparations to freedom camp (breach, non-self-contained, over capacity): $400 / $1,200
  - (i) failing or refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer: $600 / $1,800
  - s 20C: altered or fraudulent warrant card or certificate, or refusing or giving false information: $600 / $1,800.
  - The $800 fee is offence (e) only: damage/interference, or "depositing waste in or on the area while freedom camping". Camping in a prohibited/restricted area or non-self-contained vehicle is $400.
- **Council:** The page says fees "can vary in fee amounts from $400 to $1200, which are set by the New Zealand Government." (Quote; note $1,200 is the max fine, not the fee, in the regulations; flag.) Council "may also be liable for prosecution". No permits, booking or paid sites: not published. Pay online via council portal.
