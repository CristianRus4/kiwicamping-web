# Southland District Council: freedom camping facts

- Council: Southland District Council (Te Anau, Manapouri, Riverton, Tuatapere and the wider district; Bluff skipped as asked)
- Date read: 2026-10-21
- Method: plain curl on the council's freedom camping pages and the council-hosted Freedom Camping Bylaw 2015 PDF (text via pdftotext; the Schedule 2 maps are images, viewed at low resolution as page images); WebSearch for context. No block met.
- Headline for the writer: **Te Anau, Manapouri, Riverton and Tuatapere have NO council freedom camping site.** The bylaw maps for all four show the whole township as "Camping Prohibited" and the council's site list has no entry for them. Council sites are small-town sites elsewhere (Dunsdale, Monkey Island, Curio Bay campground, Winton, etc.).

## 1. Bylaw
- **Fact:** Southland District Council Freedom Camping Bylaw 2015, in force 12 December 2015 (repealed the Camping Control Bylaw 2012). A map was replaced 5 October 2020 (W2 Waikawa).
  - URL: https://southlanddc.govt.nz/assets/Bylaws-policies-and-strategies/bylaws/Freedom-Camping-Bylaw-2015.pdf. Read 2026-10-21.
  - Quote: "This Bylaw is the Freedom Camping Bylaw 2015." and "This Bylaw comes into force on 12 December 2015."
- **Fact (2026):** The bylaw is under review, formal consultation "late 2026".
  - URL: https://southlanddc.govt.nz/make-it-stick/under-review/freedom-camping-bylaw-review/ (read 2026-10-21).
  - Quote: "The Freedom Camping bylaw is due for review. ... Formal public consultation will take place late 2026".
- Council pages: https://southlanddc.govt.nz/services/freedom-camping/ , .../freedom-camping-need-to-know/ , .../freedom-camping-locations/ .

## 2. Self-contained vehicles
- **Fact:** Bylaw permits freedom camping on local authority land unless prohibited; designated sites (maps) apply specific rules. Prohibited in urban zones, car parks, boating areas, playgrounds, sports grounds, cemeteries, walking tracks and signposted lookouts.
  - Quote (cl 3): "Any person may freedom camp in any local authority area unless it is restricted or prohibited by". Schedule 1: "freedom camping is prohibited in the following - (a) Urban zones; and (b) Council-controlled car parks; and (c) Council-controlled boating areas (including any wharf, jetty, boat ramp, boat marshalling area); and (d) Playgrounds; and (e) Sports grounds; and (f) Cemeteries; and (g) Walking tracks; and (h) Signposted lookouts".
  - Council page quote: "most towns have designated sites for self-contained camping. Some towns have sites that permit all types of camping".
- **Fact:** Bylaw self-contained definition is the old NZS 5465:2001 warrant (the 2015 text predates the 2023 Act). Quote: "complies with New Zealand Standard 5465:2001, as evidenced by the display of a current self-containment warrant". Council page also says "A current self-contained warrant must be displayed in the window."
- **Fact (blue/green warrants), council review page:** Quote: "freedom camping must now be in self-contained vehicles, although tents are still permitted / self contained vehicles must be certified" and "There will be a transition period for certification from blue warrants to green warrants. All warrants must be green by 7 June 2026." (The page is a review page, written in 2025 or early 2026; the date 7 June 2026 has passed as of today.)

## 3. Tents and non-certified vehicles
- **Fact:** Sites that "permit all types of camping" (council pages): Dunsdale (non-self-contained allowed), Orepuki Monkey Island ("non self-contained camping is allowed"), Thornbury ("non self-contained camping is allowed"), Haldane and Lumsden (green areas, no vehicle restriction stated), Otautau Arboretum (green site). Tent permissions at these sites are not stated per site; the council's interactive map has a "Tents allowed camping area" legend (map not read).
- All other designated sites: "in a certified self-contained vehicle only ... camping in tents, cars, and vehicles or caravans that are not certified self-contained is not allowed at this site".
- Te Anau, Manapouri, Riverton, Tuatapere: no site (see section 5).

## 4. Nights, hours, numbers
- **Fact:** Default for designated areas: seven nights in any 30 days unless the schedule says otherwise; and not more than one month in any three months.
  - Quote (cl 4): "a maximum of seven nights in any 30 day period, or such other period as is specified in Schedule 2" and (cl 5) "must not freedom camp in any local authority area for more than one month in any three months."
- Site rules from the council location pages are in section 6. Generator hours: "limit generator use to between 8am-9pm". Open fires prohibited "except in a safe fire facility provided by Council"; dogs "must be on a leash". Arrival/departure times and per-site vehicle counts: not published.
- Dunsdale closes each winter. Quote: "the Dunsdale Recreation Reserve closes for winter from late April until late September or October depending on ground conditions". Fortrose blue site: self-contained only, 1 August to 30 November each year.

## 5. Prohibited and restricted areas
- **Fact:** Urban zones are prohibited. Bylaw Schedule 2 maps (images, read at low resolution) for **Te Anau Township (T1), Manapouri (M1), Riverton (R2) and Tuatapere (T4)** each show only red "Camping Prohibited" shading over the town and no camping site. Tuatapere map has one small blue icon (looks like a campground; unread).
  - Council location list for camping has no page for Te Anau, Manapouri, Riverton or Tuatapere. The council map lists toilets and dump stations in those towns (e.g. "Manapouri Camper van dump station", Te Anau Ivon Wilson Park toilet, Riverton toilets): these are facilities, not camping sites. Manapouri Frasers Beach toilets are listed but camping there is not offered by the council in the lists read.
  - Search extract (2021 Southland Times, via WebSearch, not read): a Te Anau-area prohibition was introduced for inappropriate camping and littering.
- Other towns' maps also show red prohibited township areas with a few green/blue/orange site areas (low-resolution read of the PDF thumbnails; not itemised here, use section 6).
- Curio Bay (council camping ground page): "you can camp at the camping ground area only".
- Stewart Island/Rakiura: map S1 all prohibited as read (hand check).

## 7. Fees and infringement
- **National (confirmed from legislation, read 2026-10-21):** Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023 (2023/136), in force 13 July 2023, made under s 43 Freedom Camping Act 2011. URL: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2023/0136/latest/LMS853112.html (PDF read: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2023/136/en/2023-06-12.pdf). Local authority areas (s 20(1)), fee / max fine:
  - (a) camping in breach of bylaw prohibition or restriction: $400 / $1,200
  - (b) using a motor vehicle that is not self-contained, other than where permitted: $400 / $1,200
  - (c) failing to display a warrant card: $200 / $600
  - (d) self-contained vehicle with more people than certified: $400 / $1,200
  - (e) interfering with or damaging the area, flora, fauna or structure, or depositing waste: $800 / $2,400
  - (f)-(h) making preparations to freedom camp (in breach, non-self-contained, over capacity): $400 / $1,200
  - (i) failing or refusing to leave when required by an enforcement officer: $600 / $1,800
  - s 20C: altered or fraudulent warrant card or certificate, refusing or giving false information: $600 / $1,800.
  - The $800 fee is offence (e) only. Camping in a prohibited or restricted area or in a non-self-contained vehicle is $400.
- **Council:** Bylaw cl 9: "liable to the penalties prescribed by any regulations made under the Act." Free to camp at sites: no fee stated on pages read. Prior consent: apply "at least 10 days in advance" (cl 7).
