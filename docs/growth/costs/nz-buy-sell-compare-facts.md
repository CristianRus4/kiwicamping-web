## Part 1: Buying a campervan in NZ

### Registering in your name (change of ownership)
- Buyer must tell NZTA "immediately" when they buy a vehicle; the online change is free; seller should do it "straight away, online at the time of sale". NZTA gives NO deadline in days in the extracts (a "7 days" figure is NOT supported). Delay can leave the previous owner liable for licence fees. [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/buying-or-selling-a-vehicle/vehicle-ownership and https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/buying-or-selling-a-vehicle/selling-a-vehicle (2026-10-21)
- Paperwork is form MR13B. For individuals the seller can only check a NZ driver licence as ID; a buyer without a NZ driver licence must go to an NZTA agent; with no valid ID the registered person is recorded as INCOMPLETE. [S] same vehicle-ownership page (2026-10-21)
- Overseas visitor registering a bought vehicle / NZ address rule: NOT found on any official page. A backpacker guide says take passport or licence to a PostShop or NZTA agent and complete MR13B, but that is informal and I did not read it (backpackerboard, blocked). GAP.
- Temporary import (your own foreign vehicle) is a different process: form MR2C, overseas visitor's vehicle under 18 months, ACC levy only plus agent admin fee, inspection required. [S] https://nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/importing-a-vehicle/exceptions/importing-a-vehicle-temporarily/ (2026-10-21)

### WoF (warrant of fitness)
- Light vehicles registered on or after 1 Jan 2000 currently need an annual WoF. NZTA does not set the inspection price (inspectors do); NZTA's label charge is $4.16 per label. A garage WoF price range is NOT published by NZTA. [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/warrants-and-certificates/warrant-of-fitness (2026-10-21)
- Change from 1 Nov 2026: light vehicles registered on or after 1 Nov 2019 move to a WoF every 2 years; 1 Nov 2013 to 31 Oct 2019 move on 1 Nov 2027; vehicles 14 to 26 years old stay annual (no change); vehicles registered before 1 Jan 2000 move from 6-monthly to annual on 1 Nov 2026. Light rental vehicles move from 6-monthly CoF to annual. Infringement fee for a WoF expired more than 2 months rises from $200 to $350. Published 16 April 2026. [F] https://vehicleinspection.nzta.govt.nz/news/changes-to-light-vehicle-inspections-from-1-november-2026 (2026-10-21). Note: a typical backpacker campervan (Hiace, 2000s) is in the "14 to 26 years" band = annual WoF.
- Recently imported (used Japanese import etc.) vehicle: must pass border inspection and entry certification; the certifier issues the WoF (light vehicles) and it cannot be registered until pre-registration is done. [S] https://nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/importing-a-vehicle (2026-10-21)

### Licence (rego) fees and ACC
- 12-month petrol private car: $172.97 for licences starting before 1 July 2026 and $181.45 for licences starting on or after 1 July 2026 (includes licence fee, ACC levy, online admin fee and GST; online admin fee $8.66 vs $11.99 at an agent). One extract also says diesel/electric private car 12 months is $241.59 (that figure may be the old pre-July table). [S, conflicting] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/licensing-rego/vehicle-fees/licensing-fees (2026-10-21). Check the live NZTA fee page before publishing. A motorhome-specific rego fee: NOT found.
- ACC motor vehicle levy: petrol pump levy 6 cents/litre; light passenger petrol rego levy $56.75 excl GST (may be superseded by the 1 July 2026 rate). [S] https://www.acc.co.nz/about-us/our-levies-2/paying-levies-if-you-own-or-drive-a-vehicle (2026-10-21). The ACC page itself loaded empty for my script.
- ACC covers injury (stated by the ACC levy page extract); whether visitors are covered: NOT confirmed. GAP.

### Road user charges (RUC) for diesel
- RUC applies to diesel, electric and other vehicles not taxed at source; pay in advance in units of 1000 km. Light diesel (2 axles up to 3500 kg): $76 per 1000 km incl GST in the NZTA factsheet and the April 2024 rate schedule. That rate is possibly stale; the live rates page was not read. Online transaction fee $12.44, agent $13.71 (older factsheet). [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/road-user-charges/ruc-rates-and-transaction-fees and https://www.nzta.govt.nz/resources/factsheets/38 (2026-10-21)
- RUC licences belong to the vehicle; sell any unused RUC with the vehicle. [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/road-user-charges/about-ruc (2026-10-21)

### Self-containment (green warrant)
- Blue warrants no longer count for freedom camping since 7 June 2026; green warrant only. Certificate plus a green warrant card displayed in the vehicle; details are in the national SCV register so officers can check on the spot. Issuer: a Certification Authority (the PGDB is the regulator). Valid 4 years. [F] https://www.pgdb.co.nz/self_contained_vehicles/private_vehicle_owners/ and https://www.camping.org.nz/plan-your-trip/self-containment-certification (2026-10-21)
- Requirements: toilet permanently fixed to and usable within the vehicle (portable toilets no longer qualify), plus water supply, wastewater, ventilation, sink and rubbish storage systems. A vehicle's size alone does not decide eligibility. [F] same PGDB page (2026-10-21)
- Cost: Certification Authority sets its own inspection fee (not published centrally) PLUS a $120 (incl GST) SCV levy paid to the Certification Authority. [F] same PGDB page (2026-10-21)
- A fixed-toilet conversion estimate of $1,188 to $1,825 comes from a 2023 MBIE consultation (old, from a technical advisory group member). [S] MBIE discussion document (2026-10-21)
- What it allows: freedom camping on public land (council land, DOC land) where self-contained vehicles are permitted; it does not apply in campgrounds, holiday parks or private property. A DOC "self-contained vehicles only" sign means only certified vehicles may stay overnight. [F] PGDB page and https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/freedom-camping/ (2026-10-21)
- Selling-side note: Travellers Autobarn NZ lists "Green sticker Self Containment" and says its Hiace Hitops are available as a self-contained option; "length of registration will be negotiated". [F] https://www.travellers-autobarn.co.nz/toyota-hiace-campervans-for-sale (2026-10-21)

### Trader vs private sale
- Dealers must be on the Motor Vehicle Traders Register, comply with the Consumer Guarantees Act (CGA) and Fair Trading Act, and attach a Consumer Information Notice (CIN: history, price, odometer, money owing). "As is where is" does not remove CGA rights with a dealer. [F] https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/help-product-service/cars/finding-the-right-car/buying-car-from-dealer (2026-10-21)
- Private sale: seller need not be registered, show a CIN, or comply with the CGA or Fair Trading Act ("buying privately gives you very few rights"; CGA applies only to businesses). A private seller still must not mislead you; you may claim via the Disputes Tribunal (the Motor Vehicle Disputes Tribunal does not deal with private sellers). [F] https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/help-product-service/cars/finding-the-right-car/buying-car-privately and https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/help-product-service/cars/solving-issues-private-seller and https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/help-product-service/cars/before-buying-used-car (2026-10-21)
- Cooling-off: NOT found on Consumer Protection's pages (no statutory cooling-off confirmed for used vehicles). Travellers Autobarn (a licensed dealer) offers its own 3-day 100% money-back for a serious mechanical issue (it can fix it first). [F] https://www.travellers-autobarn.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/TAB_NZ_Sales_Terms_and_Conditions_2025.pdf (2026-10-21)
- Someone selling more than 6 vehicles a year may count as a trader (Motor Vehicle Sales Act). [S] extract of Consumer Protection guidance (2026-10-21). Insufficiently verified: I read no page stating the number.

### History and finance checks
- PPSR: money owing on a car means you risk losing it even if undisclosed in a private sale. Search by registration number, chassis number or VIN (VIN best). Fee $2 excl GST = $2.30 incl GST per search; search results kept 14 days; you confirm a legitimate reason. [F] https://ppsr.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/paying-ppsr-fees/schedule-of-fees/ , https://ppsr.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/searching-the-ppsr/motor-vehicle-search/ , https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/help-product-service/cars/before-buying-used-car (2026-10-21)
- If you buy from a trader that does not disclose a registered security interest, you take the vehicle free of it; that protection does NOT apply to private sales. [S] Consumer Protection extract (2026-10-21)
- Consumer Protection's checklist also names NZTA's written-off/damaged-vehicle check, water-damage info, stolen check, odometer checks, independent inspection. [F] before-buying-used-car page (2026-10-21). The NZTA written-off page itself was not readable. Motochek (paid bulk service) $0.184 incl GST per query. [S] https://nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/how-the-motor-vehicle-register-affects-you/requesting-register-information/motochek/motochek-fees (2026-10-21)

## Part 2: Selling a campervan in NZ

- Seller must tell NZTA "straight away", ideally online at the time of sale; free. Needs your NZ driver licence and the buyer's full name and address; if you are a company or have no NZ licence, contact NZTA. Phone 0800 108 809 or info@nzta.govt.nz (from an NZTA OIA response). Legal basis cited: Land Transport Act 1998 s 247. No days-based deadline or penalty stated in the extracts. If you don't report, you may get the buyer's fees or fines (tolls, speeding). If the rego was overdue, you owe fees up to the date you tell NZTA. [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/buying-or-selling-a-vehicle/selling-a-vehicle (2026-10-21)
- Check the buyer has registered before handing over keys; you can check online. [S] same page.
- Plates stay with the vehicle when sold (personalised plates are separate). [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/licensing-rego/number-plates (2026-10-21)
- Unused rego refund: NZTA says it can refund unexpired licence fees only if you cancel the registration and hand in the plates (do this only if the vehicle goes off the road permanently); the admin fee is not refundable; refunds are at the Registrar's discretion. A plain sale does NOT trigger a refund (I found no NZTA statement that it does). Your task description assumed a refund on sale or export: this is not confirmed. [S] https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/vehicle-registration/cancelling-your-registration and https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/licensing-rego/getting-your-vehicle-licence-rego (2026-10-21)
- RUC: RUC licences belong to the vehicle, so sell unused RUC with it. Off-road RUC refund possible if the vehicle is sold or deregistered, destroyed, or exported; claim at least $20; apply within 2 years of the licence issue date; cancelling via form MR15 needs an odometer/hubodometer reading. NZTA does not mediate reimbursement between buyer and seller. [S] https://nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/road-user-charges/ruc-refunds and https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/road-user-charges/about-ruc (2026-10-21)
- WoF when selling: no NZTA rule read that requires a seller to supply a current WoF to a private buyer. Consumer Protection tells buyers to check for a current WoF and rego. Selling dealer vs private differences: see Part 1. [F] before-buying-used-car page (2026-10-21). GAP: NZTA's own rule on selling a vehicle without a WoF.
- Finance clearing: a private seller who says a car is debt-free when money is owing may be misleading; the buyer may cancel or claim compensation; the buyer can lose the car to a lender. Seller should clear the debt first. [F] https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/help-product-service/cars/solving-issues-private-seller (2026-10-21) and [S] extract on the PPSR/buyer side.
- Selling to a dealer or wrecker, and exporting the vehicle (customs, deregistration): no official page read. NZTA pages mention export only in the RUC refund conditions. GAP.
- Travellers Autobarn buy-back: see Part 1 (NZ schedule not found). Where backpackers sell: Backpacker Board has "for sale" listings (listing pages above); I read no official or company statement about Facebook groups.

## Part 3: Comparison facts

### (a) North Island vs South Island
- Cook Strait crossing Wellington to Picton: about 3.5 hours (Interislander FAQ); Bluebridge timetable extract shows 2:00am Wellington to 5:30am Picton (about 3.5 hours). [F] https://www.interislander.co.nz/about/interislander-ferry-faqs ; [S] https://www.bluebridge.co.nz/ (2026-10-21)
- Interislander vehicle fares "from $NZ258" (price depends on sailing, fare type, how far ahead; foot passenger from around $NZ90). You can take a campervan, motorhome or caravan on "selected bookings"; add the vehicle at booking with type, height and total length (include bike racks, roof boxes, tow bars); vehicle space fills quickly, especially weekends, school holidays and summer; "booking ahead is recommended"; arriving without a booking does not guarantee a place. Change fees $20 per person, $30 per vehicle (fare conditions page). [F] https://www.interislander.co.nz/about/interislander-ferry-faqs and https://www.interislander.co.nz/book/cook-strait-ferry-fares-and-payment-options (2026-10-21)
- Bluebridge: has separate categories for Campervan, Motorhome (bus-shaped or cutaway) and others; booking change fee $20 per person and $20 per vehicle; 1.75% card fee on card bookings. No campervan fare example published on the pages I read. [F] https://www.bluebridge.co.nz/payment (2026-10-21). - Freedom camping rules do not differ by island: the Freedom Camping Act, the national green-warrant rule and the infringement fines apply nationwide; what differs is local council bylaws and DOC site restrictions. [F] PGDB page, regulations PDF, DOC page (2026-10-21)

### (b) Campervan vs car and tent
- Fines (Freedom Camping (Penalties for Infringement Offences) Regulations 2023, in force 13 July 2023; infringement fee / maximum court fine): freedom camping on council land in a non-self-contained vehicle (other than where permitted) $400 / $1,200; in breach of a bylaw $400 / $1,200; failing to display a warrant card $200 / $600 (council, conservation and LINZ land); more people than the vehicle is certified for $400 / $1,200; damaging or leaving waste $800 / $2,400; failing to leave when told $600 / $1,800; altered or fraudulent warrant card or certificate $600 / $1,800; freedom camping on conservation land in breach of a notice $400 / $1,200; LINZ land other than where permitted $400 / $1,200. [F] https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2023/136/en/2023-06-12.pdf (as made; read 2026-10-21). Amendments since then were not checked.
- Meaning of freedom camping (DOC, citing the Freedom Camping Act s 5): camping outside a designated campground within 200 m of a road or area accessible by motor vehicle, or within 200 m of the sea or a harbour (mean low water spring), or on or within 200 m of a formed road or Great Walks track; it applies to motor vehicles including campervans, tents, bivouacs and temporary structures. You may freedom camp on public conservation land except where prohibited or restricted; you cannot freedom camp in DOC scenic or recreation reserves unless a site is provided or approved; not generally permitted near Great Walks tracks; no fires; dogs not allowed in national parks when freedom camping. [F] https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/freedom-camping/ (2026-10-21)
- Rule for tents on council land: the self-containment requirement covers motor vehicles, not tents; tent camping is allowed unless a council bylaw prohibits or restricts it (councils cannot impose a blanket ban); a tent attached to a vehicle counts as part of the vehicle. [S] from MBIE fact sheets and bill documents (blocked or old), search extract 2026-10-21. Verify the in-force Freedom Camping Act text on legislation.govt.nz before publishing.
- Default for vehicles: you can only freedom camp on public land if the vehicle is certified self-contained with a green warrant, unless the site is designated for non-self-contained vehicles. Non-self-contained vehicles can use campgrounds, holiday parks and private property. [F] https://www.pgdb.co.nz/self_contained_vehicles/private_vehicle_owners/ (2026-10-21)
- DOC campsite fees (per night; "fees listed apply to most campsites in each category, check individual pages"): Serviced: unpowered/tent adult $25, child $12.50; powered adult $20 to $28, child $10 to $14. Standard: adult $10 to $20 (unpowered and powered), child $5 to $10. Basic: free. Backcountry and Great Walk campsites: prices vary. Infants 0 to 4 free. There is no separate vehicle versus tent price; fees are per person, and an unoccupied vehicle or tent on a site is charged two adults. Campsite Pass: adult $295 per year [S], with a $3 per user per night fee for powered sites [S]. [F for fees] https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/facilities-and-fees/ ; [S] https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/booking-and-paying/campsite-pass/ (2026-10-21). Note: the DOC 2026/27 price change page exists but I did not read it (https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/online-bookings/doc-accommodation-price-changes--20262027/ appeared in search), so fees may be updated.
- Green warrant cost: $120 levy plus the certifier's inspection fee (not published) [F, PGDB page]. Hire company price notes: none read.
