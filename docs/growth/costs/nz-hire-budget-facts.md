### 1.1 thl group (Maui, Britz, Apollo, Mighty, MightyLITE; Cheapa and Hippie per FAQ) [F]
Source: thl Summary of Rental Conditions v16 (URL above) unless another URL is given.

- GST: rates are in NZD and include 15% GST. "Rates reset after 7 days." Rental days are calendar days; pick-up day and return day both count.
- Minimum hire: 5 days when picking up and dropping off at the same branch. Higher minimums apply per brand and are in a table that did not extract as text. Do not quote the higher minimums. FAQ page: "Minimum and maximum rental periods apply and are subject to change." https://www.maui-rentals.com/faqs
- Kilometres: unlimited; thl "recommend" a maximum 250 km a day (4 to 5 hours driving). 4WD limits exist only in Australia.
- Driver age: Mighty Campers (Highball, Double Down) and MightyLITE (HiLITE, DeLITE) 18+ with full licence. All other RV types 21+, licence valid for the whole booking. FAQ adds Cheapa Campa and Hippie (Hitop and Endeavour) at 18+. No maximum age. https://www.maui-rentals.com/faqs
- Overseas licence: licence not in English needs an accredited English translation or an International Driver Permit, and the physical licence must be shown at pick-up (photocopies not accepted). Select the licence country correctly or extra charges may apply.
- Included in rate: 24 hr On Road Assist, unlimited km, kitchen kit, general equipment, complimentary linen kit (bedding, sheets, pillowcases, tea towels).
- Liability ("Low Road" included; "High Road" reduces to $0, exclusions apply). Liability per claim, per day price, capped at 50 days per rental segment:
  - Maui all RVs: $7,500 included; High Road $67/day.
  - Britz and Apollo non-toilet/shower: $5,000; $57/day. Britz and Apollo toilet and shower: $7,500; $67/day.
  - Mighty Campers non-toilet/shower: $3,500; $52/day. Mighty toilet and shower: $5,000; $57/day.
  - MightyLITE: $3,500; $42/day.
  - Value Pack per day: Maui, Britz and Apollo toilet and shower $77; Britz/Apollo non-toilet $67; Mighty toilet $72; Mighty non-toilet $62; MightyLITE $52. Platinum Pack per day: $93, $82, $82, $72, $72 respectively (minimum charge 10 days).
  - Britz's own page shows the same: Low Road $5,000 (HiTop, Voyager) or $7,500 (Venturer, Wanderer, Odyssey, Navigator); High Road $57 or $67 per day; Value Pack $67 or $77; Platinum $82 or $93; Re-Fuelling Pack from $339 per rental. https://www.britz.com/nz/en/travel-info/liability-reduction
  - Windscreen and tyres are excluded from the High Road; Windscreen and Tyre Protection is $16/day (cap 50 days); Extended Roadside Assistance $9/day.
- Bond/deposit in NZ: no bond amount is stated in the NZ documents I read. The Low Road is described as needing a "credit card authority" (card details kept to recover amounts owed). The "Liability Deposit" debited on the day of collection is described on the FAQ for AUSTRALIAN rentals only. Do not state an NZ hold amount or how it is held. Booking deposit is $250 at booking; balance due 30 days before pick-up; bookings under 30 days need full payment.
- Card fees: Visa/Mastercard 2.46%, Amex 5.2%. No cash at branches.
- Extra driver: $4.25 per person per day, capped at 15 days. Included in Value Pack and Platinum Pack. (FAQ's "$4 per person per day, max $60" line is the older or AU-style wording; the v16 PDF is the later source.)
- Diesel Road User Charge recovery fee, collected on return by kilometres driven: $8.18 per 100 km for 4 and 6 berth toilet and shower; $8.04 per 100 km all other vehicles. Prepay option $15.00/day (4 and 6 berth) or $14.50/day (others). Britz repeats $8.04 (HiTop, Voyager, Venturer) and $8.18 (Wanderer, Odyssey, Navigator). https://www.britz.com/nz/en/travel-info/liability-reduction
- Other prices (PDF): RV Essentials Kit $155 (non-toilet and 2/3 berth toilet) or $205 (4/6 berth), per rental; camp chair $24 each; camp table $33; extra duvet $21; fan/heater $21; child/booster seat $46 each (max 2); linen exchange $42; gas bottle $36 (non-toilet) or $47 (toilet); snow chains $57 (on request at pick-up only); bike rack $50; Drop and Go Pack $109 or $145; Re-fuelling Pack $339 ("subject to change"); Flexible Change Cover $15/day; first aid kit $45 if broken seal; pet fee $325 per rental (not in Maui vehicles); public holiday surcharge $125; booking alteration $50 plus any fare increase within 60 days; change of drop-off after pick-up up to $750.
- One-way fees (Mon-Sun, minimum 5 days; extra minimums may apply): Auckland to Christchurch or Queenstown $200 (1 Apr to 30 Sep 2026) and $300 (1 Oct 2026 to 31 Mar 2027); Christchurch or Queenstown to Auckland $200 (1 Oct to 31 Mar); Christchurch to Queenstown $200 (all year); Queenstown to Christchurch $100 (1 Oct to 31 Mar). Maui FAQ lists the 1 April 2027 increases ($205, $310, $105). https://www.maui-rentals.com/faqs
- Queenstown location fee: $270 per rental ($280 from 1 Apr 2027) (Maui FAQ and PDF).
- Airport and hours: free transfers between airport and Auckland, Christchurch, Queenstown branches on day of arrival and departure. Maui FAQ: no early or late pick-up after hours. Branches closed 25 Dec 2026. Public holiday surcharge $125 applies on the observed holidays listed in the PDF (for example 26 Oct 2026, 28 Dec 2026, 1 Jan 2027).
- Unsealed roads: sealed/bitumen only, except well-maintained access roads under 12 km to recognised commercial campgrounds or major tourist attractions (and ski field access roads in the PDF wording). Not permitted at all: Skippers Road, Crown Range Road/Cardrona Valley Road, Ninety Mile Beach, Wanaka-Mt Aspiring Road beyond Treble Cone exit, Ball Hut Road, north of Colville and Waikawau (Coromandel). No ski field access roads 1 Jun to 31 Oct. Britz page: https://www.britz.com/nz/en/travel-info/road-restrictions . Breach "may void the liability option".
- Cook Strait: thl says "Ferry travel is required" between islands, you book the ferry yourself with the operator (cost depends on vehicle length), and advises booking a 7.7 m RV on the ferry irrespective of the size of motorhome reserved. FAQ suggests check-in about 60 minutes before. The ferry is not included. https://www.maui-rentals.com/faqs

### 1.3 Travellers Autobarn NZ [F]
Source: Terms and Conditions New Zealand 19 June 2026 PDF https://www.travellers-autobarn.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Terms-and-Conditions-New-Zealand-19th-June-2026.pdf and FAQ https://www.travellers-autobarn.co.nz/campervan-hire-new-zealand/faqs
- Daily rates: no price in page text. FAQ says "May to September is very low season... under $40/day! April and October are... shoulder season while November to March are peak season". Note this season split differs from Jucy's.
- Minimum hire 5 days same-city; one-way minimums 10 to 35 days; one-way fee "may apply" and varies, amount not published. Unlimited km. Rates include GST. Change of drop-off up to $500.

- Extra driver $1 per day, max $25, up to 4 extra drivers.
- Living kits (one-off): $45 for 2 and 3 berth, $75 for 4 berth (+$25 5th person); stationwagon camping kit $45. Child seat $45. Prepaid gas $35. GPS $7/day (max $140). Tablet $10/day.

## PART 2: WORKING HOLIDAY ROAD TRIP BUDGET IN NEW ZEALAND 2026

### 2a. Immigration New Zealand working holiday visa [F]
Pages read 2026-10-21 (country-specific; there is no single generic page): https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/germany-working-holiday-visa/ , .../united-kingdom-working-holiday-visa/ , .../canada-working-holiday-visa/ , .../usa-working-holiday-visa/ , .../france-working-holiday-visa/ , .../ireland-working-holiday-visa/ , .../japan-working-holiday-visa/
- Funds: at least NZD $4,200 for Germany, Canada, USA, France, Ireland, Japan. United Kingdom is different: at least NZD $350 a month. Also need an onward ticket or money to buy one, in addition to the living funds. Evidence is bank or credit card statements (page wording).
- Fee: "Cost From NZD $770" on each country page; the exact fee for your passport shows only after entering country details ("How to pay and submit"). So $770 is a "from" figure, not a confirmed total. Processing time shown: "80% within 2 weeks".
- International Visitor Levy: NZD $100 for many working holiday travellers. https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/applying-for-a-visa/fees-processing-times-and-refunds/paying-the-international-visitor-levy/ . Whether it is paid separately from the $770 was not confirmed from the page text.
- Length of stay: 12 months (Germany, USA, France, Ireland, Japan pages show 12 months), 12 or 23 months (Canada), 12, 23 or 36 months (UK). Age: 18 to 30 (Germany, USA, France, Ireland, Japan); 18 to 35 (UK, Canada).
- Work and study: can work in temporary jobs for up to the visa length (12 months on 12-month visas), study or train up to 6 months in total. No permanent job. No job offer needed. Needs full medical insurance for stay (Germany page). Must arrive within 12 months of visa issue; cannot delay or cancel.
- Employment conditions: from 20 April 2026 open work visas carry one of two conditions. The working holiday page says "You cannot operate a business on a working holiday visa." Open visas cannot employ others. https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/upcoming-changes-to-employment-conditions-for-open-work-visa-holders/
- Chest X-ray and medical needed if total stay over 12 months; police certificates if total stay 24 months or more.

### 2d. Camping costs
- DOC campsite fees [F]: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/facilities-and-fees/ (page has no date; read 2026-10-21; says fees "apply to most campsites... check individual campsite pages").
