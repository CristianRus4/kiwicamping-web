# Facts: Best tramping and Great Walks apps in New Zealand 2026

All facts checked 2026-10-21. Prices NZ$. Ratings: NZ App Store (iTunes lookup, country=nz) unless marked Play. "unverified" = do not state as fact.

Writer rules: KiwiCamping is NOT a topo or navigation app (ASO notes: it "does not claim complete topo-map or offline navigation capability"). Say a competitor lacks something only where this sheet says "listing does not mention" (a statement about the listing).

## 1. Official sources first (DOC, LINZ, MSC)

**DOC Great Walks and huts** (no national DOC app: NZ App Store search "department of conservation" returned only local DOC apps, Pūkete Taiao o Tāmaki Makaurau and Kōkopu o te Waihou; Play not checked)
- DOC compares "eleven" Great Walks (Waikaremoana, Tongariro Northern Circuit, Whanganui Journey, Abel Tasman, Heaphy, Paparoa, Routeburn, Kepler, Milford, Hump Ridge, Rakiura). https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/things-to-do/walking-and-tramping/great-walks/compare-great-walks/
- "Bookings are open for stays to 30 June 2027." Hump Ridge: "bookings are made through the Hump Ridge Trust website". https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/things-to-do/walking-and-tramping/great-walks/ ; same date on https://www.doc.govt.nz/online-bookings ("All bookings are first-come first-served. We do not have waiting lists... Not all facilities can be booked.")
- 2026/27 opening days were 12 to 21 May 2026, 9:30 am, with a virtual queue (DOC media release 26 March 2026, https://www.doc.govt.nz/news/media-releases/2026-media-releases/ready-set-naturing-doc-great-walk-hut-and-campsite-bookings-open-from-12-may/). 2027/28 dates: unverified.
- Season dates differ by track. Milford (Clinton Hut page): "In the Great Walks season (1 November 2026 – 30 April 2027 last departure 28 April 2027) Bookings are required"; outside it (1 May to 31 October) "Bookings are not required - first come, first served". https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/things-to-do/huts/clinton-hut/ . Tongariro Northern Circuit: season 23 October 2026 – 30 April 2027, "Bookings are required for huts and campsites year-round"; the same page says one section "is closed due to the 2025 Tongariro fire and will not be available for the 2026/27 Great Walk season" while also saying the Great Walk "will be available for the 2026/27 walking season" (DOC's wording is inconsistent: tell readers to check the track page). https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/central-north-island/places/tongariro-national-park/things-to-do/tracks/tongariro-northern-circuit/
- Great Walks prices (DOC pricing page, no year printed, incl GST; summer = October to April; "DOC staff will conduct random checks... to ensure people have paid the correct fee"; NZ-resident eligibility criteria linked). Huts adult/child, NZ vs international: Milford $106/$53 vs $152/$76; Routeburn $88/$44 vs $132/$66; Abel Tasman $55/$28 vs $84/$42; Heaphy $44/$22 vs $66/$33; Lake Waikaremoana $35/$18 both. Kepler/Routeburn campsites $28/$14 vs $42/$21. https://www.doc.govt.nz/about-us/our-role/managing-conservation/recreation-management/great-walks-management/pricing/
- Backcountry Hut Pass: "gives access to most serviced and standard huts, as well as most campsites outside serviced huts"; 12 months; adult (18+) $160, child (5-17) $80, infant free; buy online, up to one month before first night; start date cannot be changed; 48-hour cancel if unused; non-bookable huts stay first come first served ("does not give you preference to a bunk"). Some huts excluded. Te Araroa walkers use the Te Araroa Trail Pass. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-in-a-hut/booking-and-paying/backcountry-hut-pass/
- Backcountry Hut Tickets: Standard adult $15, youth (5-17) $7.50; Serviced adult $25 "Some seasonal fees", youth $12.50; infant free; "you can't pay for bookings with Hut Tickets". "From 1 November 2026, Backcountry Hut Tickets... will be available through DOC's online booking system"; paper tickets accepted until 1 November 2027; "Electronic hut tickets are not bookings." https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-in-a-hut/booking-and-paying/backcountry-hut-tickets/
- CONFLICT with our live guide doc-huts-new-zealand-guide (says every serviced hut bookable "since 1 October 2026"): DOC's tickets page says "All Serviced Huts will be bookable before the end of 2026". Do not repeat either; say "DOC is moving serviced huts onto the booking system".
- DOC weather page: MetService provides "brief New Zealand mountain forecast" and "extended New Zealand mountain forecast"; NIWA "provides weather forecasts for national parks"; "If you are going into the mountains check the New Zealand Avalanche Advisory as well as weather forecasts." https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/know-before-you-go/weather/

**LINZ (official topo maps)**: you can "download individual map images for the 1:50,000 or 1:250,000 scale topographic map sheets"; digital data via the LINZ Data Service; paper maps from bookshops and outdoor stores. https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/maps/where-find-maps . LINZ phone app: none seen on that page (unverified).

**Mountain Safety Council (MSC)**: Plan My Walk is MSC's free app and website. Site says "over 3,000 tracks, huts and campsites" (listing says 3,500+: conflict, say "3,000 or more"). Maps "including LINZ Topo and satellite maps"; alerts "provided by the Department of Conservation and local councils"; forecasts "provided by MetService"; a free account is needed for trip plans. https://www.mountainsafety.org.nz/discover/plan-my-walk (last edited October 2026).

Per-app facts (listing text unless stated)
- **Plan My Walk.** Does: track, hut and campsite search, alerts, weather, gear lists, custom tracks, trip planner that sends details to an emergency contact. Data: DOC and council alerts, MetService forecasts, LINZ Topo (MSC page). Offline, developer FAQ (https://planmywalk.nz/help, read headless): "Does the app function offline and how do I access my trip plan when I'm in the hills? Yes, if you have an eligible Satellite plan. If not, you can download a PDF of your trip plan so you can save this to your device and access it offline." Satellite: One NZ and Spark customers with eligible phone and plan can reach the app by satellite; "TXT and satellite-enabled apps only"; needs line of sight to the sky (MSC page). Best for: planning and sharing a trip. Fair con: offline maps are not described by the developer, so pair it with a topo app.
- **NZ Topo50 (Right Place).** "complete offline topographic map coverage" of one island from "LINZ Topo50"; "no need for phone or wifi signal"; includes DOC huts, campgrounds, tracks, Te Araroa, weather forecasts, GPS, track recording, GPX import. Pro: maps ship inside the app. Con: $6.99 per island (two purchases for both).
- **NZ Topo Maps (Rajan Fernandez).** "Topo250 and Topo50 maps direct from the LINZ data service"; download only what you need; "DOC huts and campsites with the latest alerts"; DOC hunting areas; GPX import. Extra sheets and routes are paid IAP.
- **iHikeGPS NZ.** "official LINZ New Zealand topo maps"; "Even in remote areas with no cell signal" ; one price, no subscription; 28 ratings.
- **TrailTreks.** "Free NZ topo maps that work offline"; "Every DOC hut, campsite and track", DOC alerts notifications, MetService severe weather warnings, location sharing, Te Araroa sections. Maps save as you browse, or download an island or the country. First release 2025-09-30, 8 ratings.
- **Trig.** "Topo50 maps for the whole country... downloaded to your phone for offline use", every DOC hut, track, campsite; Plus adds recording, navigation, trip planning, Apple Watch. Released 2026-10-12, iOS 26.4+, 1 rating: call it new, do not rank it on reviews.
- **Te Araroa app.** "The official Te Araroa app, from the Te Araroa Trust. Free." (developer's own words); trail topo maps stored on the phone; huts, alerts, weather, tides. The 3,000 km trail only.
- **Gaia GPS.** Global; listing never mentions New Zealand (maps page has an "Australia/New Zealand/Asia" group). NZ layers and what Premium unlocks in NZ: unverified.
- **AllTrails.** "over 500,000 trails around the world"; offline map downloads need Plus; community-contributed trails and reviews. NZ track count, DOC alerts: not in listing (unverified).
- **Mountain-Forecast.com.** "more than 11,000 summits worldwide"; site lists NZ peaks (Aoraki/Mount Cook etc., https://www.mountain-forecast.com/countries/New-Zealand); offline is a subscriber feature. Not an official NZ source.
- **MetService app.** Official weather service. The NZ listing (checked 2026-10-20) lists no mountain forecasts; DOC links to MetService's website mountain forecasts.

## 4. Safety facts (official only)
- DOC Land Safety Code (https://www.doc.govt.nz/safety): 1 Choose the right trip for you; 2 Understand the weather ("It can change fast. Check the forecast and change your plans if needed."); 3 Pack warm clothes and extra food; 4 Share your plans and take ways to get help ("Telling a trusted person your trip details and taking a distress beacon can save your life"; use the Outdoors Intentions form or Plan My Walk); 5 Take care of yourself and each other.
- MSC (https://www.mountainsafety.org.nz/learn/skills/communications): "we recommend always taking another communication device, especially on trips into remote areas"; a 406 MHz PLB "must be registered with the Rescue Coordination Centre of New Zealand (RCCNZ)"; "The NZ emergency 111 number cannot accept calls from data enabled apps"; PLBs can be bought or hired. MSC (https://www.mountainsafety.org.nz/learn/skills/leaving-trip-intentions): "A phone call isn't enough when leaving your intentions."
- NOT verified (sites blocked): AdventureSmart's own pages (Cloudflare 403), Emergency Mobile Alert and 111 TXT pages. Do not state EMA or TXT details.
