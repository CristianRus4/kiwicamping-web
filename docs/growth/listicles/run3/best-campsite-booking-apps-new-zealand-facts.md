# Facts: Best campsite booking apps and sites in New Zealand 2026

All facts checked 2026-10-21. Prices NZ$. Ratings are NZ App Store unless stated (Google Play ratings are global-style counts shown on the Play page). "unverified" = could not be confirmed from a primary page. Source URL follows each fact. "Fetch" = page read via a page-summary tool, so wording is a close paraphrase unless in quotes.

## Headline findings for the writer
1. There is no single booking app for NZ camping. Bookings are split: DOC (web system, no app), holiday park chains (own websites), and private-land/aggregator apps (Campable, Hipcamp web, CamperMate, WikiCamps, Rankers).
2. DOC has no national camping/hut app. iTunes NZ search "department of conservation" returned only local nature apps (Pūkete Taiao o Tāmaki Makaurau, Kōkopu o te Waihou), seller Department of Conservation, and a DOC site search found only web booking. https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=department%20of%20conservation&country=nz&entity=software (checked 2026-10-21). Google Play: not searched directly (unverified).
3. DOC bookable inventory is a minority. DOC: about 950 huts, most non-bookable. https://www.doc.govt.nz/news/media-releases/2025-media-releases/naturing-made-easier-45-doc-huts-and-campsites-to-move-onto-the-booking-system/ KiwiCamping bundled data (dataAsOf 2026-08-24, DOC counts as of 2026-08-11): 177 of 287 DOC campsites and 127 of 927 DOC huts are flagged bookable (lib/data/product-facts.json). These are the app's counts, not DOC totals.

## 1. DOC booking system (bookings.doc.govt.nz) - official
- What: DOC online booking for Great Walks, huts, campsites, and lodges/cabins/cottages. "Not all facilities can be booked." Bookings open for stays up to 30 June 2027. Bookings are first come first served; no waiting lists for future seasons or full facilities. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/online-bookings/
- Account: log in to create, modify or cancel bookings (login page). https://bookings.doc.govt.nz
- Campsites: bookings required for "all serviced campsites, and some scenic and standard campsites in peak season", peak "usually 1 October to 30 April". Other campsites are first come first served; pay at the self-registration stand. "You pay for the campsite when you book." Confirmation email is your proof. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/booking-and-paying/
- Campsite fees per night (adult 18+; child 5-17 is half; infant free): Serviced unpowered $25; Serviced powered $20-28; Standard $10-20; Basic free; Backcountry and Great Walk vary. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/facilities-and-fees/
- Huts: "You'll find out if a hut needs to be booked or not on the hut web page." Non-bookable huts are first come first served. "You pay for the hut when you book." https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-in-a-hut/booking-and-paying/
- Hut window: no single national window; each hut page lists it (fetch of the hut booking page and a search found none). Treat as per-hut.
- Newer bookable sites: 22 huts and 23 campsites joined the system from Dec 2025 (43 bookable in December after two campsites deferred to Autumn 2026); fees unchanged; backcountry huts stay unlocked as emergency shelter. https://www.doc.govt.nz/news/media-releases/2025-media-releases/naturing-made-easier-45-doc-huts-and-campsites-to-move-onto-the-booking-system/
- Hut tickets: "From 1 November 2026, Hut Tickets for non-bookable backcountry huts will be sold through DOC's online booking system." (hut booking page above)
- Great Walks 2026/27 (stays 1 Jul 2026 to 30 Jun 2027): booking opened from 12 May 2026, staggered by track; North Island campsites 14 May, South Island 15 May, Tōtaranui 22 May; DOC's upgraded system "includes a virtual queue"; 11 Great Walks; Hump Ridge booked separately up to two years ahead. https://www.doc.govt.nz/news/media-releases/2026-media-releases/ready-set-naturing-doc-great-walk-hut-and-campsite-bookings-open-from-12-may/ The 2027/28 opening date is not published in what we read: unverified.
- Great Walks prices (summer huts per night, adult/child): residents and international visitors pay different rates on some tracks, e.g. Routeburn hut NZ $88/$44, international $132/$66; Milford $106/$53 vs $152/$76; Lake Waikaremoana and Whanganui same for both. DOC "will conduct random checks". Page says summer hut fees "Oct-April" without a year. https://www.doc.govt.nz/about-us/our-role/managing-conservation/recreation-management/great-walks-management/pricing
- Passes: Backcountry Hut Pass 12 months adult $160, child $80 (most serviced and standard huts and campsites outside serviced huts; some excluded). Campsite Pass 365 nights adult $295, child $147.50; up to 7 nights in 30 days at one campsite; bookable campsites still need a booking. Buy online up to a month before first use; 48-hour cancel window. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-in-a-hut/booking-and-paying/backcountry-hut-pass/ and https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/booking-and-paying/campsite-pass/
- Fees and cancellation (DOC T&Cs): no online booking fee stated in what we read (unverified that none exists). $10 service fee on bookings made through DOC staff; $10 fee when a change reduces a booking's value; cancellation fee % of booking: Great Walks 10% (48 hrs after booking, 15+ days out), 50% (14-4 days), 75% (3 days to day before), 100% (day before/day of/after); backcountry huts 10/20/30/100; campgrounds 10/20/75/100; modification and service fees non-refundable; DOC cancels and fully refunds affected bookings if it closes a facility. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/bookings-terms-and-conditions/huts-campsites-cabins-and-lodges-terms-and-conditions/
- Residency: proof needed for resident rate; failure means rate difference plus $50 admin fee per person (same T&Cs).
- CONFLICT / unresolved: the booking login page text (fetch) says "You must have a pass to reserve a campsite" and offers to add one; DOC's Campsite Pass page says passes are an optional payment option and without one you pay standard fees. Do not state that a pass is required. Verify in the live flow before saying either.
- App: none found (see headline 2). Alerts: the login page appears to offer availability alerts for a date range (fetch only, unverified).
- Best for: huts, Great Walks, DOC campsites at official prices. Cons: bookable sites are a minority; web only; Great Walks need a queue and fast action.

## 2. Holiday park chains and groups (websites; apps limited)
- TOP 10 Holiday Parks: "Our 52 holiday parks in New Zealand"; booking is via website ("BOOK NOW"); membership card "$55 for 2 years", "Save 10%" on accommodation at all TOP 10 parks (terms apply). Booking fee and cancellation terms not stated on the home page (unverified). https://top10.co.nz
- TOP 10 app (id6471460094, TOP TEN GROUP NEW ZEALAND LIMITED): free, v8.0.2 (2026-09-16), 0 NZ ratings, iPhone only. Listing: "Access your digital membership card", "View and manage your bookings", member discounts, "Explore TOP 10 Holiday Parks". Making a new booking in-app is not stated. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id6471460094
- Holiday Parks New Zealand (association): "our 230 member parks"; site offers Advanced Search and Parks Near Me; no booking flow or app described. https://holidayparks.co.nz
- Kiwi Holiday Parks, Haka and Park Hotels: not verified. kiwiholidayparks.com currently serves unrelated gambling content, so its status is unknown; no app found in iTunes NZ search "kiwi holiday parks". Do not include as recommendations; one search found no holiday park business called Haka (Haka House and Haka Tours exist but are hostels/tours). Park Hotels not searched (unverified).
- Council parks and Penny: a Waitomo District Council page says its parks use Penny (formerly KiwiCash) for bookings and payment (search summary only, unverified). App Store "Go Penny" (Kiwi Cash Limited) 2.67 from 3 ratings, v1.1.0 (2025-08-20) - purpose not verified.
- Best for: families and anyone wanting powered sites, kitchens, bookable certainty in peak summer. Cons: highest nightly cost; each chain books separately. (Cost figures: see guide camping-fees-new-zealand-2026 rather than quoting here.)

## 3. Campable (private land, motorhomes and campervans)
Full verified sheet: campable-alternatives-facts.md. Key facts: free app (App Store v17.5.0, 2026-10-19, 4.54 from 13; Play 2.63 from 57, com.campable); "No guest booking fees"; hosts pay 25% commission; card charged 14 days before; cancellation 6+ days full refund less card fee, 1-5 days 50%, same day none; certified self-contained vehicles only; "You'll need an internet connection to book". Sources: https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/campable/id1209349485 ; https://www.campable.com/payments ; https://www.campable.com/cancellation-policy ; https://www.campable.com/faq-guest
Best for: instant-book private-land stays for self-contained vans. Cons: small host-driven coverage, low Android rating (state both ratings with counts).

## 4. Other apps with booking features
- CamperMate (id465040067, Outdoria Pty Ltd): free, v5.8.0 (2026-10-15), 4.41 from 606 NZ ratings; Play 3.96 from 12,569 (1M+ downloads). Listing: "Check availability, make secure bookings"; "Find and book campsites, holiday parks and experiences" (site). Booking provider not named on campermate.com; one app review (2026-01-04) mentions "BookIt bookings", reviewer claim only. Offline: "Download maps and access crucial info... no expiration". https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id465040067 and https://campermate.com
- WikiCamps New Zealand (id582527125, WikiCamps pty ltd): NZ$7.99 one-off on App Store (site: "$9.99 (AUD) per platform"; different currency, not a real conflict), 4.47 from 137 NZ ratings, v6.3.0 (2026-10-19); Play 2.95 from 1,042. "book from 1K+ sites directly in the app"; processor not named. 12K+ NZ sites, offline mode, "No subscriptions". https://wikicamps.co.nz and https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id582527125
- Rankers Camping NZ (id582914766, Rankers.co.nz Ltd): free, v2.25.0 (2026-08-13), 4.70 from 3,322 NZ ratings; Play 3.70 from 1,771. Listing: "around 500 bookable campground options"; DOC and some council sites link to their own booking pages ("Check Availability"); free sites have no in-app booking; "takes a small commission on bookings"; "campsite info works offline from day one", detailed maps are a download. Two 2024 reviews say "10% charge for booking through rankers" (reviewer claim, unverified). https://camping-nz.rankers.co.nz/about
- NZMCA App (id1555394769): free app, membership required: $150 joining plus $102 annual ($252 first year); NZMCA Parks; 0 NZ ratings, v1.2.9 (2026-09-19); Play 3.22 from 156. Members only, certified self-contained. https://www.nzmca.org.nz/join-us
- Hipcamp NZ: web listings in NZ ("3,303 campsites", mix of private land and holiday parks, currency not stated on page, snapshot figure); added 60+ Hampshire Holiday Parks across AU and NZ (article 2026-05-20). The Hipcamp app is not in the NZ App Store (iTunes lookup country=nz returned none; country=au returned it). https://www.hipcamp.com/en-AU/d/new-zealand/camping/all and https://moderncampground.com/australia/hipcamp-adds-more-than-60-hampshire-holiday-parks-across-australia-and-new-zealand/
- Freedom camping permits: no national permit app found. Freedom camping on council land is by bylaw; some places need a permit, e.g. Horowhenua Kimberley Reserve by permit (application form via council), https://www.horowhenua.govt.nz/CommunityPlaces/Halls-Recreation-Sport/Parks-Reserves-Recreation/FAQs-Parks/Camping-in-the-Horowhenua-District (search summary). Do not list a permit app.

## 5. KiwiCamping (ours) - what to claim
- Free to download, with in-app purchases. iPhone only. 3.75 from 4 NZ ratings, v2026.9.6 (2026-09-24), iOS 26+. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/camping-map-nz-kiwicamping/id6746952595
- Not a booking marketplace. It shows "Book now" and "Check availability" links for BookIt stays and tours (free in the app) and for DOC campsites and huts that DOC flags as bookable; the booking itself happens on the partner or DOC site (code: Kiwi/Camp/KiwiPlacesRepository.swift; link destination for DOC sites not independently confirmed).
- Offline: listing says the place directory is stored on the phone; "Live map imagery, directions, forecasts, availability and external links can still require a connection." No offline map tiles. Availability is never shown live.
- Best for: deciding where to stay (DOC, huts, freedom camping notices, dump stations, trip planning) and then booking on the official site.
