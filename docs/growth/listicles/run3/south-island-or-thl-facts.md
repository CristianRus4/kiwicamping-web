## 1. thl Roadtrip app - what it is (baseline)
- Publisher: Tourism Holdings Limited (thl), seller on App Store; copyright "© 2014 GeoZone Ltd". Free. iPhone and iPad (App Store page), iOS 12+, 172 MB; languages English, French, German, Simplified Chinese. Version 8.3.9, 2025-06-22. No in-app purchases listed on the page. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/thl-roadtrip/id906468182
- Android: Play title "thl Roadtrip" (package nz.co.britz), 3.98 from 402 ratings, 100K+ downloads, updated 29 Aug 2025. Ratings differ iOS 4.65 (122) vs Play 3.98 (402): report both with counts.
- Description (App Store): GPS location shows "the nearest campsites and holiday parks, (free and paid), petrol stations, free wifi, supermarkets, dump stations, public toilets, LPG filling stations, laundromats"; "how to" vehicle operation videos; community tips; "real-time road alerts" if roads are closed; covers NZ and Australia. Last release note: new campsite filters for powered sites, unpowered sites and same-day booking.
- Britz page (official): show-through video of your camper ("It's a requirement that you watch this video before you get here", part of pick-up), CamperHelp "how to" videos, "pre-book campsites, holiday parks, attractions and experiences directly within the App" along your itinerary, offline maps ("the maps work offline"; must be saved beforehand; video transcripts downloadable), reviews and feedback, iOS and Android. https://www.britz.com/nz/en/travel-info/britz-roadtrip-app
- Mighty Campers page: walk-through video of the exact model booked, CamperHelp library, drop-off "step-by-step" reminders, GPS location search, "powered by CamperMate", "book them directly through the App" for activities (does not say campsites), iPhone and Android. https://www.mightycampers.com/nz/en/travel-info/travel-app . The two brand pages differ slightly on whether campsites are bookable in app: unresolved, say "campsites, holiday parks and activities per Britz".
- Brands: Britz and Mighty pages describe the app; the Apollo travel-info page is titled "thl Roadtrip App" (search result title only); Maui, other thl brands and any other hire company: unverified. Do not claim every hire brand supplies it.
- What the sources do not describe (neutral phrasing only): DOC hut and campsite booking, DOC conservation-land freedom-camping rules, weather forecasts, trip planning with driving times. Say "not described on thl's pages", never "lacks".
- Data: "powered by CamperMate" (Mighty page), so its place data and the CamperMate app share a source. How often the thl app receives that data after a June 2025 update: unverified.

## 4. Hire-specific cautions (what we can say)
- Follow your hire company's own instructions; the Britz page makes the show-through video a pick-up requirement. Only the thl app is named for that on the pages read.
- Freedom camping and self-contained rules apply to hire vehicles as to others; see guides below rather than restating.
- Fairness: thl's app is the hire companies' own service, free and unrelated to camping-app subscription pricing. Reasons to add another app: wider place filters, fee and rating detail, a trip plan. Reasons to keep thl's: vehicle videos and returns help.
