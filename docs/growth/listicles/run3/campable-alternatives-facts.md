# Facts: Alternatives to Campable in New Zealand

All facts checked 2026-10-21. Prices NZ$. Ratings are NZ App Store unless stated; Google Play counts are from the Play page. "unverified" = not confirmed from a primary page. Reuse notes from /Users/cristian/Developer/Webs/.growth-lanes/research/k-listicles-apps-B.md, k-listicles-rankers-campermate.md, k-listicles2-wikicamps-campervan.md (checked 2026-10-20) were re-verified for price, rating and version today.

Framing: Campable is a private-land booking marketplace for self-contained motorhomes and campervans, not a general campsite directory. "Alternatives" therefore fall into three kinds: other directories (CamperMate, WikiCamps, Rankers, KiwiCamping), official booking (DOC), and other booking routes (holiday parks, Hipcamp, NZMCA). Be fair: Campable is still best for one thing (instant-book private land).

## 1. Campable today (the baseline)
- App Store "Campable", CAMPABLE 2021 LIMITED, free, v17.5.0 (released 2026-10-19), 4.54 from 13 NZ ratings, iPhone only (iOS 15.1+), English only, 125 MB. No in-app purchase list on the page. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/campable/id1209349485
- Android exists: com.campable, "Campable: Motorhome Camping NZ", 10K+ downloads, updated 20 Oct 2026, 2.63 from 57 ratings. The App Store listing is iPhone only; the website FAQ says "Campable app for iPhone and Android". Earlier research (2026-10-20) said Android was unconfirmed; it is confirmed today. Ratings conflict (Apple 4.54 from 13 vs Play 2.63 from 57): report both with counts, do not guess a cause. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.campable&hl=en_NZ&gl=NZ ; https://www.campable.com/faq-guest
- What it is: motorhome and campervan stays on private land: "Farms, orchards, vineyards and more, each with a local host"; listing also names sports clubs and resorts; "hundreds of sites"; 16 regions Northland to Southland. https://www.campable.com ; listing description
- Booking: instant book on some places; others "ask the host first, and you're only charged when they say yes". Card charged 14 days before the stay. Pay in the app/website via Stripe. https://www.campable.com ; https://www.campable.com/faq-guest ; https://www.campable.com/cancellation-policy
- Price: app free. "No guest booking fees" and "The price you see is the price you pay"; sample nightly "from" prices $25 and $40 on the home page (not an average). Hosts pay 25% commission on the nightly price, 15% on extras (listing is free). Card fee about 2.9% + 30c comes off before commission. https://www.campable.com/payments
- Cancellation: 6+ days: full refund less card fee; 1-5 days: 50% (after card fee); on the day: no refund; host cancels: full refund plus fee covered; peak season (20 Dec-15 Jan and public holiday weekends Jan-Easter) stricter. If a host cancels shortly before arrival and Campable cannot find you an alternative, "Campable may charge the host up to NZ$50 for the inconvenience and pass it on to you" (not for same-day bookings): so the guest can receive up to $50 in compensation, it is not a guest fee. https://www.campable.com/cancellation-policy
- Rules: "Certified self-contained only: your vehicle needs a current green self-containment warrant"; hosts may accept a portable toilet; pets are the host's decision. https://www.campable.com/faq-guest
- Newest features (v17.5.0 release notes): chat with your host in the app, optional home-cooked dinner or breakfast from some hosts, host tools on phone, map shows dump stations, LPG refills and Cook Strait ferries, "Pet friendly" filter, listing translation. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/campable/id1209349485
- Offline: "You'll need an internet connection to book"; arrival details "work offline for when there's no signal". Offline maps: not mentioned. https://www.campable.com
- Data: host-submitted listings; "Every host is checked before their land goes live" (listing). Reviews from travellers who stayed.
- Pros: instant booking on real availability, quiet places not in public directories, host contact, flat per-night price with no guest fee. Cons: only private-land sites ("hundreds"), self-contained vehicles only, small rating base on iPhone (13), low Android rating, no offline map mentioned.

## 2. Alternatives (pick 5-6; suggested order below)

### A. KiwiCamping (ours) - directory plus trip planner
- Camping Map NZ: KiwiCamping (id6746952595), Cntxt Limited. Free to download, with in-app purchases (say only this). iPhone only (iOS 26+). 3.75 from 4 NZ ratings (few ratings). v2026.9.6 (2026-09-24). https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/camping-map-nz-kiwicamping/id6746952595
- Does: map of campsites, DOC huts, freedom camping areas, holiday parks, dump stations, day-use areas; filters (water, toilets, showers, power, dogs, vehicle access, fees); trip planner with driving route, distances and times, dates, checklist, calendar sync; collections; 21 road-trip routes and 50 guides (listing). "More than 4,500 useful places" (listing).
- Data: DOC (campsites, huts, tracks, freedom camping notices which are DOC conservation-land notices, not council bylaws), WikiCamps-sourced dump stations and day-use areas (product-facts.json dataAsOf 2026-08-24); BookIt partner stays and tours.
- Bookings: not a marketplace. BookIt stays and tours (free in the app) and DOC places flagged bookable show "Book now" links that go to the partner or DOC site (Kiwi/Camp/KiwiPlacesRepository.swift). Not a substitute for Campable's instant booking of private land.
- Offline: "The New Zealand place directory is stored on your phone"; "Live map imagery, directions, forecasts, availability and external links can still require a connection." No offline map tiles.
- Best for: planning a whole road trip around DOC and free options. Cons: new app, 4 ratings; iPhone only; no private-land marketplace; no live availability.

### B. CamperMate Australia & NZ (id465040067, Outdoria Pty Ltd)
- Free, v5.8.0 (2026-10-15), 4.41 from 606 NZ ratings; Play 3.96 from 12,569, 1M+ downloads (global page). 27 languages. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id465040067 ; https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=nz.co.campermate&hl=en_NZ&gl=NZ
- Does: campsites, holiday parks, freedom camping spots, dump stations, toilets, Wi-Fi, supermarkets; reviews and photos; saved lists; "Check availability, make secure bookings"; deals. Offline: "Download maps and access crucial info without an internet connection"; "no expiration"; v5.8.0 adds satellite view. App Store listing says "5 million downloads". https://campermate.com lists "Same Day Booking" and "Instant Book" badges.
- Data: users can "submit updates" and suggest places in the app (listing); the thl Roadtrip app is "powered by CamperMate" (https://www.mightycampers.com/nz/en/travel-info/travel-app). Data origin beyond that: unverified.
- Cons from reviews: a late-2025 redesign drew complaints (7 of 15 reviews dated 2025-26 in a 50-review sample, "Bring back the old app"; earlier research, RSS 2026-10-20 not re-read); older reviews mention ads (Play listing shows "Contains ads", checked 2026-10-20). Review claims, not verified.
- Best for: free all-round NZ and Australia coverage with offline maps.

### C. WikiCamps New Zealand (id582527125, WikiCamps pty ltd)
- NZ$7.99 one-off on the App Store (website states AUD $9.99 per platform); 4.47 from 137 NZ ratings; v6.3.0 (2026-10-19); Play 2.95 from 1,042. "No subscriptions or hidden fees." https://wikicamps.co.nz ; https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id582527125 ; https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=au.com.angryrobot.wikicampsnz&hl=en_NZ&gl=NZ
- Does: "over 12,000 sites across New Zealand"; community reviews, photos, fee info; trip planner; offline mode ("access maps and up to three of your planned trips even without phone signal" per site); "Book and Save": "book from 1K+ sites directly in the app" (booking provider not named).
- Best for: the most detailed community-sourced site database. Cons: paid; community data can be out of date; booking is a small share of listings.

### D. Rankers Camping NZ (id582914766, Rankers.co.nz Ltd)
- Free, v2.25.0 (2026-08-13), 4.70 from 3,322 NZ ratings (largest NZ rating base); Play 3.70 from 1,771, 100K+. https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id582914766
- Does: "over 1500 places you can stay" (listing) or "over 1000 campsites" (website); places where the local community has said overnight stays are welcome; "over 108k traveller reviews" each read by a person; "around 500 bookable campground options"; "campsite info works offline from day one", map packs downloaded for offline. Also on iPad, Android and web. https://camping-nz.rankers.co.nz/about
- Money: free to the user; holiday parks pay to be listed; commission on bookings and activity deals "at no extra cost to you"; "primarily funded by campervan hires". Two 2024 reviews say "10% charge for booking through rankers": reviewer claim, unverified.
- Best for: confident legal, welcome-sites with local notes. Cons: one filter at a time (reviews, 2020 and 2025), smaller coverage than WikiCamps.

### E. DOC booking system (official)
- bookings.doc.govt.nz books Great Walks, huts, campsites, lodges; no app. Most of about 950 huts are first come first served. Fees: Serviced campsites $25-28 an adult, Standard $10-20, Basic free; Campsite Pass $295 adult, Backcountry Hut Pass $160 adult. Cancellation fee table and $10 staff service fee in DOC T&Cs. https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/online-bookings/ ; https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/stay-at-a-campsite/facilities-and-fees/ ; T&Cs: https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-stay/bookings-terms-and-conditions/huts-campsites-cabins-and-lodges-terms-and-conditions/
- Best for: conservation-land camping and huts at official prices. Cons: no private land; web only.

### F. Holiday park booking (TOP 10 and parks direct)
- TOP 10: "52 holiday parks"; booking on website; membership "$55 for 2 years", "Save 10%"; member app (free, 0 NZ ratings, v8.0.2) shows membership card and bookings. https://top10.co.nz ; https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/id6471460094
- Holiday Parks New Zealand association: "230 member parks". https://holidayparks.co.nz
- Best for: powered sites and facilities with certainty. Cons: most expensive; chain-by-chain booking.

### G. Hipcamp (web; optional)
- NZ web page lists "3,303 campsites" (mix of private land and holiday parks; currency unstated); Hipcamp added 60+ Hampshire Holiday Parks across AU and NZ (2026-05-20). Hipcamp app not in NZ App Store (iTunes lookup country=nz empty). Host commission 15% / 12.5% (earlier research, support page 403 today: unverified). https://www.hipcamp.com/en-AU/d/new-zealand/camping/all
- Closest in spirit to Campable (private land) but broader, tents and glamping included. Verify NZ coverage near your route before recommending.

### H. NZMCA App (members only)
- Free app, membership $150 joining + $102 a year; Travel Directory (2,400+ places per the join page; 2,200 in another section: conflict on the same page); NZMCA Parks $5 per adult per night (earlier research, unverified today); 0 NZ ratings; Play 3.22 from 156. https://www.nzmca.org.nz/join-us
- Best for: self-contained members who travel often. Cons: membership cost and self-contained requirement.

## 3. When Campable is still the better choice
- You want a private-land stay (vineyard, farm, orchard) with instant booking and a host to meet.
- You travel in a certified self-contained vehicle and want a quiet paddock not in directories.
- You want the exact price up front with no guest booking fee; peak-season cancellation is stricter than the standard terms, so check dates.
- Not better when: you need tent or non-self-contained sites everywhere, free sites, DOC huts, an offline map or a whole-trip planner.
