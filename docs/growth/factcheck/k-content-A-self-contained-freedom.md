Key legal facts established:
- Infringement fees (PEN Sch 2): council land s20(1): bylaw breach $400; non-self-contained vehicle $400; no warrant card $200; over certified occupancy $400; damage/waste $800; preparations offences $400; refusing to leave $600. Conservation land s20A: notice breach $400; no warrant card (SC-restricted land) $200; damage/waste $800; refusing to leave $600. LINZ land s20B: $400. Maximum fines are exactly 3x each fee ($1,200 / $600 / $2,400 / $1,800).
- FCA Sch 1AA: commencement date 7 June 2023; period 4 starts 18 months later (7 Dec 2024) and during period 4 old-standard (blue) certification counts only for vehicles "not owned by a rental company" (cl 6(2)(d)). EXT reg 3: "The end date of period 4 is 7 June 2026." PGDB-MR and DOC-FC: blue warrants not recognised "From Sunday 7 June" 2026. (Note: read literally, EXT makes 7 June 2026 the last day of period 4; the official PGDB/DOC position is that blue stopped counting from 7 June. The site follows the official position, so marked CORRECT; moot in October 2026.)
- FCA s10: on council land freedom camping is permitted by default in a tent or a self-contained vehicle; a non-self-contained vehicle only where a s11A bylaw (or other legislation) permits. FCA s15: conservation land permitted unless a s17 notice or other enactment restricts/prohibits. No offence of "non-self-contained vehicle" on conservation land except via a notice restriction ($400 under s20A(a)).
- FCA s5(1): "freedom camp" = camp (other than at a camping ground) within 200 m of an area accessible by motor vehicle, within 200 m of the mean low-water springs line of any sea or harbour, or on/within 200 m of a formed road or a Great Walks Track. s5(2)(c): resting/sleeping at the roadside to avoid driver fatigue is not freedom camping. s5(3): "camping ground" includes "any site at which a fee is payable for camping at the site".
- FCA ss29-30: hirer may agree in the rental service agreement to pay; card debit only if the company sends the notice + agreement within 5 working days; refund if hirer not liable at a court hearing.
- FCA ss37-38: seizure/impoundment on council or conservation land; vehicle only if necessary for public health, public safety, significant flora/fauna, access, or most appropriate to prevent ongoing offence; officer must first direct the person to stop and warn of seizure.
- SCVR reg 13: eight requirements (water supply, fixed toilet, grey+black wastewater, sink draining to tank, clean water loading, hygienic disposal to outfall, secure rubbish storage, ventilation); reg 14: operate 3 days for maximum certified occupants. Levy $104.35 + GST = $120 incl GST (PGDB-PO).
- PGDA s87X: certificate valid up to 4 years, expires on date shown, ceases if material modification to critical elements or revoked. s87ZA: register enables public, certification authorities and enforcement officers to check certificate status.
