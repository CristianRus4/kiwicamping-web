## Overseas driver licence (/guides/overseas-driver-licence-new-zealand/)
- 18 months for car licences from last arrival, 12 months from 1 Nov 2026: CONFIRMED. NZTA Factsheet 72 (July 2026): "You can currently drive in New Zealand using an overseas car licence for up to 18 months from your last date of entry. From 1 November 2026, this reduces to 12 months. You can drive for up to 12 months on overseas motorcycle or truck licence."
- People who arrived 1 Nov 2024 to 31 Oct 2026 keep 18 months: CONFIRMED. Land Transport (Driver Licensing) Amendment Rule 2024 (SL 2024/222, as at 7 May 2026), Schedule 1AA Part 2 cl 2: applies to a person who "arrived in New Zealand between 1 November 2024 and 31 October 2026"; "Clause 88AA continues to apply to the person". Clause 88AA: may drive until "the person has remained in New Zealand for a continuous period of 18 months".
- Page and FAQ are consistent (18 months to 31 Oct 2026 arrivals, 12 months after). No change needed.
