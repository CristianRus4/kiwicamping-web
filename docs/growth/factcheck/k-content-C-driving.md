# Fact-check verdict C — driving and road-rules pages (as at 20 October 2026)

Method: each page read in full. Claims checked against NZTA pages (live nzta.govt.nz returns an Incapsula bot block to all automated fetchers, so NZTA content was read through Wayback Machine captures dated 2025–Oct 2026 and NZTA text quoted in search indexes), the Land Transport (Road User) Rule 2004 (legislation.govt.nz PDF reprint), the Land Transport (Driver Licensing) Amendment Rule 2024, Community Law Manual, NZTA Journeys bulletins, Interislander and news reports. Advice and opinion (for example "keep the first day short") is not graded. Claims that are only experience or anecdote are marked UNSUPPORTED only where they are written as fact.

Key sources used:
- S1 NZTA "Driving on New Zealand roads" (visitors), Wayback 5 Oct 2026: https://www.nzta.govt.nz/travelling-on-our-roads/visitors-and-new-residents/driving-on-nz-roads — "You can currently drive using an overseas car licence for up to 18 months from your last date of entry ... From 1 November 2026, this will be reduced from 18 months to 12 months"; "You can use an overseas motorcycle or truck licence for up to 12 months"; "full overseas car licence, you can drive a car or light vehicle with a gross laden weight of up to 6000kg. This is the same as a New Zealand class 1 licence"; "If your overseas licence isn't in English, you must also have an accurate English translation or an international driving permit (IDP). The IDP can be current or expired up to 6 months (for car licences only). You must also carry your current overseas licence"; "carry a translation from an approved translator".
- S2 Community Law Manual, general driving offences: https://communitylaw.org.nz/community-law-manual/chapter-32-driving-and-traffic-law/general-driving-offences — extension revoked 1 November 2026; "If you last arrived in New Zealand between 1 November 2024 and 31 October [2026], the 18-month limit will continue to apply to you"; arrivals from 1 Nov 2026 get 12 months; extension "does not apply to driver licences for vehicles other than cars"; limit "resets every time you enter New Zealand". Matches Land Transport (Driver Licensing) Amendment Rule 2024 (SL 2024/222) transitional clause: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2024/0222/latest/whole.html
- S3 Land Transport (Road User) Rule 2004, reprint PDF: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2004/427/en/2017-07-01.pdf — r 2.1(2) slow driver must move left "as soon as is reasonably practicable" to let following traffic pass (no vehicle count); r 5.4(1) 90 km/h rigid tow of a motor vehicle, r 5.4(2) 50 km/h non-rigid tow of a motor vehicle, r 5.4(4) 90 km/h towing a trailer; r 5.5 heavy motor vehicle 90 km/h; r 7.6 child restraint under 7; trailer rule "must not use a motor vehicle to tow a trailer designed for use as a human abode while any person is carried in that trailer".
- S4 NZTA give-way rule change material (giveway leaflet / Road Code insert): https://nzta2.cwp.govt.nz/assets/resources/give-way-leaflet/docs/general.pdf — from 25 March 2012: right-turners give way to opposing left-turners; at uncontrolled T-intersections the terminating road gives way to all traffic on the continuing road.
- S5 NZTA Road Code alcohol and drugs limits: https://www.nzta.govt.nz/roadcode/general-road-code/about-limits/alcohol-and-drugs-limits/ — 250 mcg/L breath, 50 mg/100 mL blood for 20+; zero under 20.
- S6 NZTA Road Code safe loading and towing (and NZTA glovebox guide): https://nzta.govt.nz/roadcode/general-road-code/about-your-vehicle/safe-loading-and-towing-for-light-vehicles — 90 km/h with rigid connection; 50 km/h with rope; following gap 2 s → 4 s; Class 1 learner/restricted: combined weight ≤ 4,500 kg; glovebox guide: towbar download "about 10% of the weight of the trailer and load", "It is against the law for passengers to ride in a caravan".
- S7 Setting of Speed Limits Rule 2024 / Beehive "Speed limit reduction reversals begin": https://www.beehive.govt.nz/release/speed-limit-reduction-reversals-begin — reductions since 2020 on specified road types reversed by 1 July 2025 (43 of 49 state highway sections returned to previous limits); variable school speed limits required by 1 July 2026.
- S8 Average speed cameras: Matakana Road enforcement from 1 Dec 2025; SH1 Sanson–Foxton switched on Oct 2026 (https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/10/14/average-speed-cameras-switched-on-at-stretch-of-lower-north-island-highway/); NZTA rolling out further sets.
- S9 NZTA Journeys bulletin: https://www.journeys.nzta.govt.nz/traffic-bulletins/state-highway-2-remutaka-hill-closed-due-to-high-winds — SH2 Remutaka Hill closed to all traffic in severe gales (e.g. 17 Sept 2025, gusts 141 km/h); high-sided vehicles warned.
- S10 Interislander vehicle length page: https://www.interislander.co.nz/book/vehicle-length — extra charges by length; "We will check your vehicle measurements when you check in"; no height-based pricing. Bluebridge asks for accurate length; says height restrictions are not really an issue.
- S11 Land Transport Act 1998 ss 88–90 (Community Law demerit page): demerit points are recorded against "a person"; a person without a current NZ licence who reaches 100 points is disqualified from holding or obtaining a licence for 3 months. Police: camera-detected offences carry no demerit points.
