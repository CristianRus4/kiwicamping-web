# Research sources

Prices and rules were checked on 12 August 2026. Article-level source links are also rendered at the end of the relevant guide.

All foreign-currency columns are mechanical conversions from a New Zealand-dollar source amount. The site identifies the rate date and explains that a bank or payment provider can apply a different rate or fee.
